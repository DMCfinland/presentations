# Content Quality SEO Audit: jarvisydan.com/en/

**Audit Date:** 2026-02-11
**Auditor:** Claude Opus 4.6 (automated)
**Scope:** 5 key English-language pages
**Domain:** www.jarvisydan.com

---

## Executive Summary

Jarvisydan.com presents a visually-driven resort website with **uneven content depth** across its key pages. The homepage (~2,100 words) and activities page (~2,800 words) provide substantial content, while the spa landing page is critically thin (~280 words of unique content) due to a redirect to a holiday package page rather than presenting spa facility information. The restaurants page (~850 words) is adequate but lacks the depth competitors offer. Geo-keyword coverage is **inconsistent** -- "Lake Saimaa" appears frequently but critical terms like "Finnish Lakeland," "Rantasalmi," and "Savonlinna" are underused or absent from key pages. E-E-A-T signals are present but **fragmented** -- the 1658 heritage story, Green Key certification, and Restaurant Solitary's ranking are strong assets that are underexploited. The site has a strong unique value proposition (lakeside spa resort next to Linnansaari National Park) but fails to articulate it consistently across pages.

**Critical finding:** The `/en/spa/` URL -- which would be the primary landing page for anyone searching "Järvisydän spa" or "Lake Saimaa spa" -- redirects to a holiday package sales page with minimal content. The actual rich spa content (~3,000 words describing pools, saunas, facilities, pricing) lives at `/en/lake-spa/` but is not the page users land on from `/en/spa/`.

**Impact:** Users and search engines arriving at `/en/spa/` get a thin package page instead of comprehensive spa facility information. This likely causes:
- High bounce rates from organic spa searches
- Poor ranking for spa-related keywords
- Missed opportunity to showcase the resort's strongest differentiator

**Note:** The actual Lake Spa page (`/en/lake-spa/`) has excellent content:
- ~3,000 words of detailed facility descriptions
- 5 pools described with temperatures and features
- 6 saunas with temperature and atmosphere details
- Sustainability story (500-year sinker logs, solar power, geothermal)
- Clear pricing table
- Opening hours
- But this content is buried behind navigation, not at the expected `/spa/` URL

### Missing Geo-Keyword Opportunities

**"Rantasalmi"** -- The resort is physically located in Rantasalmi. This town name should appear in:
- Every page's introductory paragraph (establishes physical location for Google)
- Schema markup (LocalBusiness address)
- Accommodation descriptions ("our villas in Rantasalmi")

**"Finnish Lakeland"** -- This is the #1 tourism marketing term for the region internationally. Its near-total absence is a significant SEO gap. Should appear:
- Homepage H1 or H2
- Meta descriptions
- At least once on every service page

**"Savonlinna"** -- As the nearest well-known city (~60 km), this keyword drives substantial search traffic. Should appear:
- Accommodation page (distance/transport info)
- Activities page (opera cruise connection)
- Restaurants page (dining alternative/complement to Savonlinna)
