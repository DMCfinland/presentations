Järvisydän Resort

The Heart of Lake Saimaa

Where We Are

Järvisydän Resort is located in Rantasalmi, in the heart of the Finnish Lakeland on the shores of Lake Saimaa — Europe's fourth largest lake.

  • Helsinki (HEL) 3.5 h by train
  • Savonlinna 40 min by car
  • Linnansaari Nat. Park 30 min by boat

Surrounded by 4,400 km² of pristine lake, islands, and ancient forest. Linnansaari National Park — home to the endangered Saimaa ringed seal — is our neighbor, reachable by boat directly from the resort.

One resort. Everything you need. On the most beautiful lake in Finland.

Linnansaari
National Park

One of Finland's most treasured national parks lies just 30 minutes by boat from Järvisydän. A pristine archipelago of forested islands, hidden coves, and crystal-clear waters.

Boat excursions depart directly from the resort — guided tours, private cruises, and the iconic M/S Puijo dinner cruise through the Saimaa waterway.

Saimaa Ringed Seal — one of the world's rarest seals, with only ~450 individuals. Our guided seal safaris offer a unique chance to observe them in their natural habitat.

Island experiences: Hiking trails, campfire lunch on a private island, kayak excursions through the archipelago, and sunset champagne cruises.

National Park Seal safari Boat cruises Island dining

Lake Spa

Järvisydän's Lake Spa brings together the best of Finnish wellness with the Saimaa lakefront as your backdrop. Open sky, warm water, and the silence of nature.

Outdoor heated pools overlooking Lake Saimaa — swim in warm water while watching the seasons change across the lake and forest.

Sauna World — Traditional Finnish smoke sauna, wood-burning saunas, and cold plunge pools. The authentic sauna ritual that Finland is famous for.

Treatments — Peat and herb treatments using local natural ingredients. Massage, facials, and holistic wellness packages designed around the lakeside setting.

Tavern &
TeePee Restaurant

Tavern's Fire Menu — Our signature dining experience. Seasonal ingredients cooked over open flame at your table — an interactive fire-cooking moment that brings the Finnish campfire tradition indoors. The scent of birch smoke, the crackle of flame, and flavors drawn directly from the forest and lake.

Traditional TeePee Restaurant — The resort's iconic kota restaurant. Open-flame cooking under a traditional teepee roof. Smoked meats, grilled fish, and Finnish campfire cuisine served in an atmosphere that connects you to centuries of lakeland tradition.
