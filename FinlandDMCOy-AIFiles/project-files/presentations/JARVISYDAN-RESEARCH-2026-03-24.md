## 3. LAKE SPA — DETAILS

There is ONE spa facility called "Lake Spa" (Jarvi-kylpyla). There is NO separate "Cave Spa" — the cave is an ARCHITECTURAL FEATURE inside the circulation pool.

### Marketed as: "The most ecological Lake Spa in all of Finland"

### Building & Materials
- Built partly INSIDE THE ROCKS (natural rock formations)
- Materials: Lake Saimaa water, natural stone, 500+ year-old sunken logs
- Heated ecologically: lake/geothermal energy (10 km underground piping) + solar power

### Pools (5 total)
| Pool | Temp | Depth | Features |
|------|------|-------|----------|
| Circulation Pool | +31°C | 130 cm | CAVE in the centre, massaging water jets, swim upstream/downstream |
| Whirlpool | +31°C | — | "Savonian boomerang-shaped" |
| Children's Pool | +31°C | 30 cm | Waterfall and slide |
| Outdoor Pool 1 | Unheated (lake water) | 90 cm | Real Lake Saimaa water |
| Outdoor Pool 2 | Unheated (lake water) | 70 cm | Real Lake Saimaa water |

### Saunas (6 types)
| Sauna | Temp | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Sunken Log Sauna | +70°C | Traditional Finnish steam |
| Hamami | +40°C | Mild, heated seats |
| Steam Sauna | +55°C | Marble bench |
| Infrared Sauna | +45°C | Heat-based |
| Stone Sauna | +85°C | Hottest option |
| Scenery Sauna | +70°C | 2nd floor, lake views (closed in winter) |

- All saunas are SHARED (men + women together, swimsuit required)

### Eco Features
- 75% energy from solar (April–September)
- Geothermal heating via 10 km underground piping
- Infrared water purification (reduced chemicals)
- Motion-sensor LED lighting
- No disposable plastics
- Nordic Swan Ecolabel (2018)

### Hours & Prices
- Daily 10 AM–8 PM (adults-only after 6 PM)
- Adult: EUR 33.90 / Child (under 12): EUR 21.90 / Family: EUR 77.90
- Evening Spa: EUR 52 (pre-reservation)

### Kuru Lakeside Saunas (separate, at Kuru)
- Year-round access for Kuru guests
- Outdoor hot tubs
- Private dock swimming
- Part of Kuru adults-only area

### Distance & Access
- Jarvisydan is the GATEWAY to the national park
- Boat cruise: **45 minutes each way** from Jarvisydan
- Paddling distance: ~12 km from Porosalmi (where Jarvisydan is)

## 9. KEY FACTS FOR B2B PRESENTATION

- Total accommodation types: ~20+ distinct room/suite/villa categories
- Restaurant count: 7 dining venues (1 fine dining at Kuru, 1 fire menu, 1 casual, 1 bistro, 1 bar, 1 group kota, 1 cafe)
- Spa: 5 pools + 6 saunas in one Lake Spa complex
- National park: 45-min boat ride, daily cruises May–October
- Year-round destination (winter: ice fishing, skating, skiing, stargazing / summer: cruises, seals, fishing, SUP)
- Adults-only premium tier: Kuru (separate property, Restaurant Solitary, lakeside saunas)
- Sustainability: triple-certified (STF + Green Key + Nordic Swan)
- Gateway to Linnansaari National Park + Saimaa ringed seal habitat
- Helsinki: 3.5 h drive / 5 h train+taxi

### What I Did NOT Find
- "Rinnehuvilat" English name used by resort = "Hillside Villas" (confirmed)
- No accommodation called just "Lake Villa" in the main resort — the Kuru property has "Kuru Lake Villa" and the main resort has "Lakeside Villa" (Rantahuvila)
- Bird Nest EXISTS — egg-shaped, 2-person, lake view, bookable
- NO separate "Cave Spa" — the cave is inside the Lake Spa's circulation pool
- Hotel building is called "Experience Hotel" (Elamyshotelli) in marketing, not a separate brand name
- Solitary ranking: confirmed #9 in 2025. The 2026 list has not been published yet (comes around April). The website currently says "9th best" which reflects the 2025 result.
