### What the Levi Tour presentation does well
1. Warm cream background — NOT white, NOT dark. Feels premium and inviting.
2. Elegant serif headlines (similar to Playfair Display / Cormorant Garamond) — feels editorial, not corporate
3. Generous whitespace — slides breathe, nothing feels cramped
4. Full-bleed hero photos on cover and closing slides — emotional impact
5. Left-right split layout as the workhorse — text one side, photo the other
6. Oversized stat numbers on the Welcome slide — creates instant credibility
7. Photo mosaic (2x3 grid) for activities — shows variety without clutter
8. Stacked photos (2 vertical) for accommodation categories
9. Short, descriptive body text — not bullet points, real sentences
10. Company page last — logo, tagline, description, contact. Clean and confident.

### What the Levi Tour presentation could improve
- No product examples (how many nights, what's included)
- No pricing indication or "starting from" messaging
- No map showing how to get there
- No social proof (reviews, awards, partner logos)
- No call to action beyond contact details
