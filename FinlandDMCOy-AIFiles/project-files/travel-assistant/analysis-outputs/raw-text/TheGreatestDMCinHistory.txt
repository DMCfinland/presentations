TO: All Staff, Finland DMC Oy FROM: Leadership SUBJECT: Day 1: The End of "Good Enough" and the Start of Our New Engine.

Team,

First of all I want to thank you on your work so far. You have built a great company who our clients can trust. We are slightly profitable. We have happy clients. In most businesses, this is the moment where leadership pats everyone on the back and says, "Keep doing what you’re doing."

We are not doing that.

In the technology world, "satisfaction" is the first step toward death. The landscape of Lapland tourism is shifting. The best resorts are becoming famous enough to bypass intermediaries. If we remain a "normal" DMC—manually booking hotels, fighting for small margins, and acting as a slow middleman—we will slowly become irrelevant.

We are refusing to accept that fate. Today, we are pressing the reset button. We are returning to "Startup Mode."

What This Means for You

Everything great we have today — the trust, the relationships, the reputation — is the foundation we are now going to 10x with technology. Without the platform you have built for years, we would have zero chance to pull this off.

Everyone who chooses to build this with us will own real equity in the outcome. Details next week.

The Vision: The "Standard Oil" of Finnish Travel

Our goal is no longer just to "sell travel packages." Our goal is to become the easiest, cheapest, and fastest way to book Finland. Period.

To do this, we are pivoting immediately from a Manual-First Organization to an AI-Native Organization.

We will embrace agentic AI in our workflows and build a digital travel planing tool for our clients they have never seen. Our legacy as a trusted DMC will enable us to leverage our already excisting clients and relations to move fast and become the first mover in our field. We start offering Järvisydän customers a free travel agent and by next winter we have trancended our operations so far that we can offer free tryout periods for our travel agent partners.

What This Means for You (Don't Panic, Get Excited)

I know the words "AI" and "Automation" make people nervous about their jobs. Let me be clear about what is changing:

- The Enemy is the Admin Work: We are eliminating the dull, repetitive work of manually entering reservations into different booking engines. That is a waste of your human talent.

- The Future is Human Connection: We will let the software run the "daily life" and logistics. This frees you to do what AI cannot: build deep relationships, handle complex "human" problems, and be the true experts of Finland.

- You are the Teachers: Our software is currently incomplete. It needs you. You will move from "executing bookings" to "training the engine" and stepping in when the software hits a wall.

The New Way of Working: 3 Verticals

To break down silos and move faster, we are restructuring into three clear verticals. We will preserve our decentralized culture (no micromanagement), but we demand radical visibility.

1.  Sales: (Feeding the Engine)

2.  Execution/Holidays: (Delivering the Magic)

3.  IT & Software Development: (Building the Moat)

We will have an internal AI secretary asking each of us weekly about hot topics making a summarized letter about out progress in each frontier.

The "10 Projects" Rule: From now on, everyone will know what everyone is doing. We will have a visible board of 10 Active Projects max. Each project will have a responsible name attached to it. If it’s not on the board, it’s not a priority.

Ideas are always welcome as our new strategy allows us to embark on a limitless journey of possible improvements of our services.

Our New Operating Rhythm: Ship Weekly

We are adopting a philosophy of "Tolerating Incompleteness."

We will not wait years to release a perfect system. We will ship new product updates and tools once a month. Increasing to once a week once we find speed.

- Will things break? Yes.

- Will it be perfect? No.

- That is the point. We learn faster by doing than by planning. We will use these incremental wins to shock our customers with how fast we evolve.

The Goal: Scale & Cost Leadership

We will use our legacy relationships to feed our new digital engine. As we automate, we lower our costs. As we lower costs, we can offer better prices than any other DMC. As we lower prices, we get more volume. With more volume we get more suppliers and better rates atracting more customers. This is the flywheel that will make us the market leader.

The Commitment

We are building something that has never existed in Finnish tourism: An Agentic AI Travel Partner that scales infinitely.

This path is not for the satisfied. It is for the hungry. We are looking for builders. Your hard work on this new frontier will be rewarded greatly, based on merit and our shared success.

I am asking you today: Are you committed to building the greatest DMC in history?

Let’s get to work.
