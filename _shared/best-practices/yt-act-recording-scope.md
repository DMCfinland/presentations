---
name: Finnish YT Act — Any Employee Speech Triggers Compliance
description: YT-laki co-determination applies whenever any employee's speech is captured, regardless of pilot user's role
type: feedback
source: patrick
---

**Rule:** Finnish YT-laki (yhteistoimintamenettely, Finlex 759/2004) is triggered by **who is IN the recording**, not by who initiated the system or who holds the data controller role.

If a CEO opts in to an AI meeting transcript system "as a personal tool" — but the meetings include other employees — the system is capturing employee speech. That triggers YT co-determination regardless of:
- Whether the CEO consented
- Whether the CEO is also the data controller / rekisterinpitäjä
- Whether the intent was "personal memory tool" not "surveillance"

**Why:** YT-laki protects the employees in the recording, not the initiating party. The compliance obligation attaches to the data being created, not the data controller's role.

**Exception (narrow):** If the pilot is strictly limited to meetings where NO other employees participate (e.g. CEO + external parties only), YT menettely may not apply. In practice this is a very narrow exception — most CEO meetings include at least some employees.

**How to apply:** Before designing any Finnish AI meeting/call/recording system:
1. Ask: "Who speaks in these recordings?" not "Who initiated this?"
2. If answer includes any employees → YT menettely is required before activation
3. Shadow mode (no PII stored, transcripts anonymized) may reduce scope — verify with lawyer
