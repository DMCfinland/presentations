# Advocacy / Municipal Decision Deck Pattern

## Core Distinction

Sales deck sells a **place** (atmospheric, emotive, inspires desire).
Advocacy deck sells a **decision** (data-dense, ROI-framed, removes objections).

Every slide builds toward: "Kyllä, teemme tämän."

## Mandatory Framing Shifts

| Sales deck | Advocacy deck |
|-----------|---------------|
| "Visit Finland" lifestyle images | ROI tables and comparison data |
| "Experience the magic" | "31–80× tuotto" |
| Broad appeal, low friction | Remove specific objections pre-emptively |
| Atmospheric closing | Closing with the key numbers |
| Ask: "book now" | Ask: "periaatteellinen hyväksyntä + prosessin käynnistyminen" |

## Critical Pattern: Always Frame with ROI

Never "we want X". Always "here's the ROI of X":
- ✗ "Haluamme tukea vesiliikenneyhteyteen 10–20k/kausi"
- ✓ "10–20k → suojaa 300–500k verotuloa → 15–30× tuotto"

## Critical Pattern: Loss Framing for Risk Slide

"Jos ei tehdä mitään" slide is NOT optional. Loss framing hits harder than gain framing:
- ✗ "We could grow further with your support"
- ✓ "Infra rapistuu → 500–800 000 € kokonaistappio vuodessa"

## Critical Pattern: Precedent Before Ask

Always show another municipality did it first (Lieksa-malli):
1. Establishes the norm ("others already do this")
2. Reduces perceived risk ("someone tested it, it works")
3. Reframes from "special request" to "catching up to peers"

## Critical Pattern: Don't Ask for a Decision

Close with: "Mitä tarvitaan jotta tämä voidaan viedä talousarviokäsittelyyn?"
Transfer ownership — let them name the next step. More binding than your suggested process.

## Internal Prep Deck vs External Presentation

Two decks, different tones:
- **Internal (Velimatti-style):** Confidential badge, explicit non-negotiables (✅/❌), objection scripts, meeting flow, checklist. Tone: "we have power, let's use it."
- **External (Rainer-style):** Collaborative, data-driven, ROI-focused, low friction. Tone: "we're partners implementing your own strategy."

## Custom Slide Types Used

- `.slide-goals` — ✅/❌ two-column non-negotiables
- `.slide-table` — full-width data table, no photo (for tables with 4+ columns)
- `.slide-checklist` — action item checklist with visual empty checkboxes
- `.slide-cards` — 2×2 YES Package grid (reduce friction to yes)
- `.ask-grid` — ❌ EMME PYYDÄ / ✅ PYYDÄMME explicit split
- `.script-pairs` — objection + response formatted dialogue

## Photo Strategy for Data-Heavy Decks

Many slides are table-only (no photo). Typical ratio: ~5–6 photo slides out of 10 per deck.
Assign photos to: Cover (bg), key narrative slides (T3/T4), closing (bg).
Skip photos on: table slides, checklist, cards, goals.
