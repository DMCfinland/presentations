## Purpose

This document is the governance operating manual for 1658 Holdings Oy and its 10 portfolio companies. It synthesizes Finnish company law (OYL, KPL, TilintL, TSL), HHJ board certification standards, and Kauppakamari best practices into a single reference optimized for a sole board member managing a holding structure with ~50 employees.

Use it to: draft compliant documents, hit statutory deadlines, manage conflicts of interest across companies, and maintain governance quality that withstands audits, due diligence, and potential disputes. "Just strict enough" — follow the law, optimize the workflow.

## I. LEGAL FRAMEWORK OVERVIEW

### Core Finnish Corporate Laws

| Law | Abbreviation | Governs | Key for 1658 Holdings |
|-----|-------------|---------|----------------------|
| Osakeyhtiölaki 624/2006 | OYL | Company formation, governance, board duties, shareholder meetings, distributions | Everything — the foundation |
| Kirjanpitolaki 1336/1997 | KPL | Accounting, financial statements, archiving | Financial close, retention periods |
| Tilintarkastuslaki 1141/2015 | TilintL | Audit requirements, auditor duties | Which companies need audit |
| Työsopimuslaki 55/2001 | TSL | Employment contracts, terms, termination | 50+ employees across group |
| Kaupparekisterilaki 129/1979 | KRL | Trade register filings, public records | PRH filings for all 10 companies |
| Verotusmenettelylaki 1558/1995 | VML | Transfer pricing, tax procedures | Inter-company transactions |
| Oikeustoimilaki 228/1929 | OikTL | Contract formation, validity, unfair terms | Shareholder agreements, contracts |

### Legal Minimum vs. Best Practice

| Dimension | Legal Minimum | HHJ Best Practice | 1658 Holdings Target |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Board meetings/year | 1 (for AGM decisions) | 6-12 for operating cos, 4 for holding | 6 operating, 4 holding, 2 property |
| Board minutes content | Decisions + votes (OYL 6:6) | + rationale, risks, action items | HHJ standard — audit-ready |
| Financial review | Sign annual FS (KPL 3:7) | Monthly/quarterly at board level | Quarterly for all, monthly for large |
| Risk management | No explicit requirement | Annual risk register, quarterly review | Annual assessment, top-10 risk list |
| Board evaluation | Not required | Annual self-assessment | Annual per company, December |
| Conflict documentation | Note when voting conflict (OYL 6:4) | Proactive register, standing agenda item | Full register, reviewed annually |
| Document archiving | KPL retention periods | Permanent for governance docs, organized | Centralized `documents/` per architecture |

### For 1658 Holdings:

- **Start at HHJ standard** for the 3 operating companies (Holdings, Finland DMC, Järvisydän). Property companies can follow simplified governance.
- **Patrick as sole board member** makes HHJ discipline more important, not less — there's no peer to catch gaps. The minutes ARE the governance record.
- **Structural conflicts** (same person on multiple boards) require documentation discipline beyond what a single-company board needs.

### Critical Deadlines — Quick Reference

| Deadline | Date (Dec 31 FY) | Consequence of Missing | Law |
|----------|------------------|----------------------|-----|
| Financial statements signed | **30 April** | KPL breach; personal liability for board | KPL 3:6 |
| Corporate tax return | **30 April** | Late filing penalties; tax surcharges | VML 7§ |
| AGM held | **30 June** | OYL breach; resolutions challengeable (OYL 5:26) | OYL 5:3 |
| PRH FS filing | **2 months after AGM** | Registration default; may block future filings | OYL 8:10 |
| PRH board changes | **Viivytyksettä (without delay)** | Outdated register; transaction problems | KRL 14§ |
| Employer monthly filings | **5 days from payment** | Late filing penalties | Tulorekisteri |

### Batch Processing Strategy

For 10 companies, batch similar activities:

1. **January week 1:** All property company board meetings (3-4 cos, simple agendas, one day)
2. **January week 2:** All operating company board meetings (3 cos, substantial agendas)
3. **April production line:** Sign all FS in one day — property cos first, operating cos next, Holdings last
4. **May-June cascade:** Subsidiary AGMs first (so dividends are decided) → Holdings AGM last (receives dividends before its own AGM)
5. **October batch:** All budget approvals + inter-company agreement renewals in one week

### For 1658 Holdings:

- **Schedule subsidiary AGMs before Holdings AGM** — dividend cascade requires subsidiaries to decide and pay first
- **Property companies** need only 2-4 board meetings/year — consolidate with operating company meetings
- **Dormant/shell companies** need minimum 1 board meeting (for annual FS) + AGM = 2 events/year
- **Set all meeting dates for the year in January** — prevents drift

## III. DOCUMENT TYPES & REQUIREMENTS

### Validation Framework

Every document type uses a three-tier validation system:

| Level | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 🔴 **RED** | Legal requirement — must pass | Fix before signing/filing |
| 🟡 **YELLOW** | HHJ best practice — should pass | Address to meet governance standard |
| 🟢 **GREEN** | Excellence standard — nice to have | Differentiates in due diligence |

---

### 1. Board Meeting Minutes — Hallituksen kokouksen pöytäkirja

**Legal basis:** OYL 6:6, 6:3, 6:4

**🔴 RED — Must include:**
- Sequential number, date, place (or "remote meeting")
- All attendees listed with roles; absent members noted
- Quorum confirmed: >50% of board present (OYL 6:3)
- All members given opportunity to participate (OYL 6:3 §2 — critical for written resolutions)
- Each matter: the issue (asia) + the decision (päätös)
- Esteellisyys noted: conflicted member identified, did not participate (OYL 6:4)
- Dissenting opinions recorded verbatim if requested (OYL 6:6)
- Signed by chairman; countersigned by at least one member present (if >1 member)
- Financial decisions include specific figures and legal basis

**🟡 YELLOW — HHJ standard:**
- Opening/closing times; method of meeting (in-person/remote/hybrid)
- Agenda circulated 5+ business days in advance
- Decision rationale documented (why, what alternatives considered, what risks)
- Action items with owner name and deadline
- Previous meeting follow-up reviewed
- Related-party transactions: arm's length confirmation documented
- MD report summarized or attached

**🟢 GREEN — Excellence:**
- Each decision cross-references specific OYL section
- Risk assessment for significant decisions documented
- Draft circulated to attendees for review before signing
- Stored in structured archive with consistent naming

> **For sole board member (Patrick):** Minutes discipline is MORE important, not less. No peer checks your work. The minutes are the only evidence of informed decision-making if liability questions arise.

**Template location:** `_shared/templates/board-minutes-template.md` (to be built)
**Naming:** `{prefix}-corp-hallitus-ptk-{number}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`

### 2. Shareholder Meeting Minutes — Yhtiökokouksen pöytäkirja

**Legal basis:** OYL 5:23, 5:3, 5:16-5:20

**🔴 RED — Must include:**
- Sequential number, date, place
- Chairman (kokouksen puheenjohtaja) + minutes keeper + minutes reviewer(s) identified
- Shareholder list: all present/represented, shares and votes per shareholder
- Proxies documented (valtakirjat referenced or attached)
- Each resolution recorded with voting result
- Contested votes: exact count for/against/abstain
- Meeting notice compliance confirmed (method, timing per yhtiöjärjestys + OYL 5:16-5:20)
- For AGM: all OYL 5:3 mandatory items addressed (FS adoption, profit distribution, discharge, elections)
- Discharge (vastuuvapaus) recorded separately for board and MD
- Articles amendments: 2/3 qualified majority confirmed (OYL 5:27)
- Signed by chairman + reviewed/countersigned by pöytäkirjantarkastaja

**🟡 YELLOW — HHJ standard:**
- Board's proposal for each item recorded before resolution
- Dividend proposal includes explicit tasetesti (OYL 13:2) and maksukykyisyystesti
- Auditor presence noted at AGM
- Representation percentage stated
- Meeting documents listed as appendices

**For sole shareholder companies:** AGM can be brief and written, but still document all mandatory items. No separate reviewer needed. Annual discipline point — don't skip it.

**Naming:** `{prefix}-corp-yhtiokokous-ptk-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`

### 3. Board Resolution Without Meeting — Hallituksen päätös ilman kokousta

**Legal basis:** OYL 6:3 §2

**🔴 RED — Must include:**
- Clearly labeled as "decision without meeting"
- Sequential number consistent with board minutes
- ALL board members given opportunity to participate (this is the most critical requirement)
- Each member's position recorded (approved/dissented/abstained)
- Method of gathering decisions documented (email chain, circulation)
- Signed by chairman + countersigned by member (if >1 member)

**🟡 YELLOW — HHJ standard:**
- Reason for deciding without meeting noted
- Background memo provided (not just resolution text)
- Minimum 3 business days review time for routine matters
- Confirmed at next regular board meeting

> **HHJ policy:** Only use for routine/administrative matters. Strategy, major contracts, personnel, financial restructuring → require a formal meeting with discussion.

**Naming:** `{prefix}-corp-hallitus-paatos-{number}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`

### 4. Articles of Association — Yhtiöjärjestys

**Legal basis:** OYL 2:3, Ch. 16

**🔴 RED — Must include:**
- Toiminimi (company name)
- Kotipaikka (domicile municipality)
- Toimiala (field of activity)
- Amendment: 2/3 qualified majority at shareholder meeting (OYL 5:27, 16:1)
- Filed with PRH — amendment effective only upon registration (OYL 16:5)
- No provisions contrary to mandatory OYL provisions (OYL 1:9)

**🟡 YELLOW — HHJ standard:**
- Board composition range specified (min-max members)
- Financial year stated explicitly
- Lunastuslauseke (redemption clause) for family/group control (OYL 3:7)
- Suostumuslauseke (consent clause) if board approval needed for transfers (OYL 3:8)
- Holding company: toimiala broad enough to cover group management, financing, property
- Review every 3 years or upon significant business change

---

### 5. Shareholders' Agreement — Osakassopimus

**Legal basis:** OikTL 228/1929 (contract law — NOT regulated by OYL)

**Key point:** Agreement is binding only between parties, NOT on the company itself (OYL 5:29). Cannot override mandatory OYL provisions.

**🔴 RED:** All parties identified (name, ID, address); shares specified; governing law stated; dispute resolution specified; signed and dated.

**🟡 YELLOW — HHJ standard:**
- Transfer restrictions (pre-emption, tag-along, drag-along)
- Deadlock resolution mechanism
- Valuation method for transfers
- Board nomination rights per ownership
- Dividend policy framework
- Non-compete and confidentiality
- **Update trigger list:** ownership change, death, divorce, new business line → mandatory review

> **For holding structures:** Agreement should address inter-company relationships, transfer pricing principles, shared services, and consolidated governance standards.

---

### 6. Inter-Company Service Agreement — Konserninsisäinen palvelusopimus

**Legal basis:** OYL 1:7 (purpose/equal treatment); VML 31§ (transfer pricing)

**🔴 RED — Must include:**
- Both parties identified (name, y-tunnus)
- Services described in detail sufficient to verify arm's length pricing
- Pricing mechanism with arm's length justification (VML 31§)
- Not violating OYL 1:7 — must serve each company's interest, not just group interest
- Board approval in each company documented in minutes
- Esteellisyys managed: if Patrick sits on both boards, conflict documented
- Signed by authorized representatives per trade register

**🟡 YELLOW — HHJ standard:**
- Transfer pricing documentation if thresholds met (VML 14a-c§)
- Comparable pricing method identified (CUP, cost-plus, etc.)
- SLAs and KPIs included
- Annual review/price adjustment mechanism
- Board minutes in BOTH companies reference the agreement and arm's length basis

> **Transfer pricing is the #1 tax audit risk in holding structures.** Document everything. Refresh benchmarking annually.

### 7. Board's Report — Toimintakertomus

**Legal basis:** KPL 3:1a, OYL 8:5

> **Note:** Pienyritys and mikroyritys are EXEMPT from statutory toimintakertomus (KPL 3:1 §4). Most of Patrick's companies qualify for exemption.

**HHJ recommends preparing a voluntary version anyway** — even one page covering: key events, performance vs. plan, significant risks, outlook, related-party summary, key personnel changes. Demonstrates governance maturity for lenders, partners, and future acquirers.

---

### 8. Financial Statements — Tilinpäätös

**Legal basis:** KPL 3:1, 3:6, 3:7; OYL 8:3, 8:10

**🔴 RED — Must include:**
- Tuloslaskelma (income statement) — current + prior year
- Tase (balance sheet) — current + prior year
- Liitetiedot (notes) — per PMA for micro/small; KPA for others
- Rahoituslaskelma (cash flow) — only if NOT pienyritys/mikroyritys
- Completed within **4 months** of FY end (KPL 3:6)
- Signed by **ALL board members** + MD (KPL 3:7, OYL 8:3)
- Filed with PRH within **2 months** of AGM (OYL 8:10)
- Auditor's report attached if audited

**🟡 YELLOW — HHJ standard:**
- Board reviewed draft with accountant in formal meeting
- Going concern explicitly assessed and documented
- Distributable equity calculated for dividend preparation (OYL 13:2)
- Related party disclosures complete

**Size category impact on requirements:**

| Category | Turnover | Balance Sheet | Employees | FS Simplifications |
|----------|----------|--------------|-----------|-------------------|
| Mikroyritys | ≤ €700K | ≤ €350K | ≤ 10 | Minimal notes, no cash flow, no toimintakertomus |
| Pienyritys | ≤ €12M | ≤ €6M | ≤ 50 | No cash flow, no toimintakertomus, simplified notes |

**Threshold rule:** May not exceed more than 1 of 3 limits on BOTH preceding AND current year.

### 9. Trade Register Filings — Kaupparekisteri-ilmoitukset

**Legal basis:** KRL 129/1979

**🔴 RED:** File changes **viivytyksettä** (without delay — practically 1-2 weeks). Correct form (Y4 for Oy changes). Supporting minutes extract attached. Correct filing fee paid.

**Filing via YTJ.fi** (electronic preferred — faster, cheaper).

**Common triggers:** AGM → board election → file board composition. MD change → file immediately. Articles amended → file with new text. Auditor change → file.

**For 10 companies:** Maintain a central PRH tracker. Pull kaupparekisteriote annually for each company and verify against actual.

---

### 10. Dividend Resolution — Osingonjakopäätös

**Legal basis:** OYL 13:1-13:9

**🔴 RED — Must include:**
- Decision by shareholder meeting (NOT board alone) — OYL 13:6
- Board proposal with explicit **tasetesti** (OYL 13:2 §1): distributable unrestricted equity confirmed
- Board proposal with explicit **maksukykyisyystesti** (OYL 13:2 §2): distribution won't cause insolvency
- Based on adopted, audited financial statements
- Amount per share, total amount, source fund (retained earnings / SVOP), payment date
- Shareholder meeting did NOT exceed board's proposal (OYL 13:6 §2)
- Minority dividend right addressed if demanded (OYL 13:7: ≥1/10 of shares → up to half of profit, max 8% of equity)

**🟡 YELLOW — HHJ standard:**
- Written solvency assessment (cash flow analysis, upcoming obligations, seasonal patterns)
- Group impact assessed: subsidiary distribution tests independent
- Tax implications documented
- Post-distribution equity position projected

> **Illegal distributions must be returned (OYL 13:4).** Board members are personally liable if solvency test violated (OYL 22:1). Take the maksukyky test seriously.

---

### 11. Employment Contract — Työsopimus

**Legal basis:** TSL 55/2001

**🔴 RED — Written statement required if employment >1 month (TSL 2:4):**
- Employer and employee identification
- Start date; fixed-term: end date + justified reason (TSL 1:3 §2)
- Trial period (max 6 months; TSL 1:4)
- Workplace, main duties
- Applicable TES (collective agreement)
- Salary and pay period
- Working hours, annual leave basis
- Notice periods
- **Non-compete (TSL 3:5, amended 2022):** requires "especially weighty reason" + mandatory compensation (≤6 months: 40% of salary; >6 months: 60%)

**For holding structure roles:** Where one person serves multiple companies, define allocation clearly — which company is primary employer, how time is tracked, which bears the cost.

---

### 12. Power of Attorney — Valtakirja / Prokuura

**Legal basis:** OYL 6:26-28; Prokuralaki 130/1979

**🔴 RED:** Identify grantor, grantee, scope, limitations, date. Prokuura: registered with PRH (KRL 14§). AGM proxy: written, dated, signed, valid for one meeting unless stated otherwise (OYL 5:8).

**🟡 YELLOW — HHJ standard:**
- Maintain a power of attorney register: who has what authority, for which company, scope limits, expiration
- Annual review and revocation of unnecessary powers
- Graduated authority levels with monetary thresholds
- **Cross-company authority mapping** for holding structure
- Immediate revocation protocol when employee leaves or role changes

## IV. HOLDING STRUCTURE GOVERNANCE

### Inter-Company Transactions

> **The fundamental tension:** When Patrick sits on the board of both 1658 Holdings and Järvisydän, his loyalty duty runs to EACH company independently. A decision benefiting Holdings at Järvisydän's expense violates his duty to Järvisydän.

**Arm's length principle (VML 31§):**
- All inter-company service agreements, management fees, and loans must be at market terms
- Transfer pricing documentation mandatory if thresholds met (VML 14a-c§)
- Document pricing methodology: comparable uncontrolled price (CUP), cost-plus, or other accepted method
- **Refresh benchmarking annually** — auto-renewal without review is a risk
- Each company's board independently approves with minutes noting arm's length assessment

### Dividend Cascade Strategy

For a holding structure where subsidiaries pay dividends to Holdings, then Holdings pays to shareholders:

1. **Subsidiaries decide first** — each subsidiary AGM resolves its own dividend
2. **Each subsidiary passes its own tasetesti and maksukykyisyystesti independently**
3. **Subsidiaries pay to Holdings** — actual payment, not just resolution
4. **Holdings AGM decides Holdings dividend** — using received subsidiary dividends as distributable equity
5. **Tax considerations:** Document structure purpose beyond tax planning (economic substance)
6. **Schedule cascade:** Subsidiary AGMs in May → payments in June → Holdings AGM in late June

### Esteellisyys (Conflict of Interest) Management

**OYL 6:4:** Board member may NOT participate in decisions involving:
- Contract between the member and the company
- Any matter where member has material interest conflicting with company interest

**Patrick's Conflict Register:**

| Role at Entity | Potential Conflict With |
|---------------|----------------------|
| CEO/Board, 1658 Holdings | Every subsidiary (parent-subsidiary transactions) |
| Board, Finland DMC | Holdings (service agreements), Järvisydän (IT/marketing services) |
| Board, Järvisydän | Holdings (dividend, capital), Finland DMC (service purchases), kiinteistöyhtiöt (rent) |
| Board, Kiinteistöyhtiöt | Järvisydän (tenant/landlord), Holdings (property value, capital) |

**HHJ protocol for sole board member conflicts:**
1. Document the conflict explicitly in minutes
2. Demonstrate arm's length terms through benchmarking
3. Consider whether decision should go to shareholders instead
4. For significant matters: obtain independent valuation or fairness opinion

**Standing agenda item at every board meeting:**
> "Does the chair have a conflict of interest regarding any agenda item?"

Even for sole-member boards, this creates documentation discipline. When making a decision involving another company, minutes note: "Chair noted that this decision involves [related company]. Decision assessed on arm's-length basis. Basis: [description]."

### Consolidated Financial Statements

**KPL 6:1 / 1:6a:** Parent must consolidate if group exceeds pienyritys thresholds on BOTH preceding and current year:
- Turnover > €12M, OR
- Balance sheet > €6M, OR
- Employees > 50

**Must exceed more than 1 of 3 to trigger consolidation.**

**1658 Holdings:** With ~50 employees already at threshold, monitor turnover and balance sheet carefully. Annual assessment required.

### For 1658 Holdings:

**Holding Company Governance Workflow:**
1. **January:** Set group governance calendar, portfolio overview, capital allocation priorities
2. **Quarterly:** Review all companies' financial performance, flag underperformers
3. **September:** Group strategy session — hold/grow/divest for each company
4. **October:** Consolidated group budget, capital allocation finalized
5. **December:** Year-end cascade, group performance review, governance quality assessment across all 10 companies

**What to watch for:**
- OYL 20:23 trigger: If any company's equity drops below 50% of share capital → board must immediately convene shareholder meeting to address
- Inter-company balance growth: If receivables/payables between group companies are growing without settlement, flag it
- Governance consistency: Are all 10 companies at the same standard, or are some neglected?

## V. GOVERNANCE QUALITY ASSESSMENT

### Scorecard Framework

Annual self-assessment per HHJ recommendation. Score each item 1-4:
- **4** = Exemplary — exceeds HHJ best practices
- **3** = Good — meets HHJ best practices
- **2** = Adequate — meets legal minimums, gaps in best practices
- **1** = Deficient — legal compliance concerns

**Category 1: Board Meetings (25% weight)**
- Minimum meeting frequency met per company type
- Meetings held throughout the year (not clustered in Q1-Q2)
- Materials distributed 5+ days before meeting
- Minutes completed within 7 days, document decision rationale
- Action items tracked with owners and deadlines, followed up at next meeting

**Category 2: Financial Oversight (30% weight)**
- FS signed by April 30 (KPL 3:6)
- Board reviewed draft FS with accountant present
- Monthly/quarterly financial review at board level
- Budget vs. actual variances discussed
- Maksukyky assessment documented (if dividends)
- Cash flow forecast reviewed; inter-company balances monitored

**Category 3: Compliance (25% weight)**
- AGM held by June 30 (OYL 5:3)
- All PRH filings current
- Tax returns filed on time
- Audit requirements correctly assessed
- Related-party register maintained
- Conflicts of interest documented

**Category 4: Strategy and Risk (10% weight)**
- Annual strategy discussion held (September)
- Risk register maintained and updated
- Long-term (3-year) planning conducted
- Budget process rigorous (not rubber-stamp)

**Category 5: Governance Practices (10% weight)**
- Board evaluation completed annually
- Vuosikello followed
- External expertise engaged when needed
- Inter-company transactions at arm's length with documentation
- Year-over-year governance improvements implemented

### Score Interpretation

| Score | Level | What It Means |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| 3.5–4.0 | Exemplary | HHJ best practice level. Ready for due diligence, audits, board certification. |
| 3.0–3.4 | Good | Meets best practices with minor gaps. Sustainable quality. |
| 2.5–2.9 | Adequate | Legally compliant but significant gaps vs. best practices. Improvement needed. |
| 2.0–2.4 | Deficient | Compliance risks, oversight gaps. Urgent improvement required. |
| < 2.0 | Critical | Legal non-compliance. Significant liability risk. Immediate remediation. |

### Annual Review Process

**When:** December board meeting or January first meeting
**How:** Patrick completes scorecard for each company, identifying strengths to maintain and items scoring < 2.5 as priority actions
**Output:** Brief governance review memo + improvement action list for the year

**Sole board member self-assessment questions (complete annually per company):**
1. Did I hold enough meetings? Properly documented?
2. Did I review financials with adequate depth, or just sign?
3. Are all conflicts of interest identified and documented?
4. Are PRH filings current? Any gaps?
5. Are inter-company transactions properly documented at arm's length?
6. Am I relying too heavily on my own judgment? Where would external perspective help?
7. For the holding structure: are all 10 companies at the same governance standard?

### For 1658 Holdings:

**Target scores:**
- Year 1: Achieve 2.5+ across all companies (establish baseline, fix gaps)
- Year 2: Achieve 3.0+ for operating companies, 2.5+ for property companies
- Year 3: Achieve 3.5+ for operating companies (HHJ-ready)

## VI. IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP

### Phase 1: Foundation (Month 1)

1. **Set up governance configuration** — Create company profiles for all 10 companies (board composition, yhtiöjärjestys provisions, signing rights, auditor details)

6. **Implement board meeting templates** — Standardize minutes format across all companies (OYL + HHJ compliant)
7. **Standardize AGM process** — Build AGM preparation checklist and materials package template

9. **Create related-party register** — Group-wide register of all related-party transactions
10. **Establish document naming** — Apply `{prefix}-{cat}-{description}-{date}.{ext}` convention to all governance documents

### Phase 3: Optimize (Months 4-6)

11. **Batch similar activities** — Schedule all property company meetings on one day, all operating company meetings on another
12. **Implement reminder system** — 2-week warnings before all statutory deadlines
13. **Track governance quality** — Use scorecard quarterly for operating companies
14. **Refine templates** — Iterate based on real usage, capture lessons learned

### Phase 4: Maintain (Ongoing)

15. **Annual governance evaluation** — December scorecard for all companies
16. **Continuous improvement** — Address lowest-scoring areas each year
17. **Consider external board members** — For the 2-3 largest operating companies, even one external member transforms governance quality
18. **Monitor law changes** — OYL, KPL, TilintL amendments; adjust practices accordingly

### Audit Requirement Thresholds

**Audit is mandatory unless ALL three are below thresholds for BOTH current AND preceding year:**

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Balance sheet total | ≤ €100,000 |
| Turnover | ≤ €200,000 |
| Average employees | ≤ 3 |

**If ANY ONE threshold is exceeded in both years → audit required.**

Also mandatory if: articles require it; company's primary purpose is holding securities; shareholder(s) holding ≥1/10 demand it at AGM.

**For 1658 Holdings:** Most operating companies and the holding company require audit. Property companies: check balance sheet (high property value → high balance sheet → may trigger audit even with zero turnover).
