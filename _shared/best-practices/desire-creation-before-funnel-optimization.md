Optimizing CTAs, forms, and conversion mechanics before creating genuine desire is a category error in luxury pre-launch marketing.

**Why:** Luxury buyers are not transacting; they are dreaming. The primary goal before bookings open is building a waitlist of dreamers, not converting immediate intent. A broken form on a desire-creating site is worse than a working form on an uninspiring site — because even with a perfect form, no one who feels nothing will fill it in. Gemini S239: "No amount of CTA optimization or trust logos can manufacture genuine desire. This content is the engine of that desire."

**Sequence for luxury pre-launch:**
1. Create desire (professional video — even 90s mood piece; hero photography)
2. Build credibility (trust signals, certifications with visual weight)
3. Capture interest (working form, email waitlist, inquiry CTA)
4. Optimize conversion (CTA placement, scarcity signals, A/B testing)

**Do not start at step 3/4 without completing steps 1/2.**

**How to apply:**
- Before any website improvement sprint on a luxury pre-launch product: ask "Do we have video/photography that makes a visitor feel something?"
- If no → video brief is the highest-ROI investment, ahead of any conversion optimization
- Applies to: nature tourism, wilderness experiences, new luxury hospitality products, any pre-launch luxury at €2,000+ price point
