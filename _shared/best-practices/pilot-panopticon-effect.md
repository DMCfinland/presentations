description: When deploying monitoring/analysis systems, start with the CEO/decision-maker as the subject — never start with a view where the CEO watches employees

---

Start AI monitoring systems with the senior leader (CEO/decision-maker) as the SUBJECT of the system, not as the observer watching employees.

**Why:** Gemini advisory on Järvisydän transcript pipeline: if the first feature built is "Patrick watches Markus's meetings" or "CEO watches staff performance", the entire project dies due to the Panopticon effect. Staff perceive surveillance → adoption fails → project cancelled. Starting with "the system helps Markus track HIS OWN promises" → no surveillance concern → natural expansion possible.

**How to apply:** For any new AI analysis/monitoring system:
1. Identify the most senior person who will benefit most from SELF-knowledge
2. Build the first feature to give that person insights about THEMSELVES
3. Only after pilot validates the value → expand to cross-team or upward visibility
4. Frame to staff as "Muistituki" (memory aid), not monitoring

**Source:** Session 113/114, Gemini advisory, Järvisydän transcript pipeline design.
