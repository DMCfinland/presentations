## Your Mission: Review Through a Sensitive Team Lens

Imagine you're a capable hotel staff member who:
- Uses WordPress but isn't a "technical person"
- Feels nervous when seeing terms like "schema markup" or "hreflang"
- Worries about breaking the website
- Has 2-3 hours max per week for this project
- Wants to help but feels unsure if you can do it

Now review the materials asking:

### 1. Emotional Safety & Confidence
- **Does the tone feel supportive or demanding?**
- Are achievements celebrated? (e.g., "Great job!" after tasks)
- Are mistakes normalized? (e.g., "If this doesn't work, here's why...")
- Is there reassurance that tasks are safe and reversible?
- Does the reader feel "I can do this" or "This is scary"?

### 2. Cognitive Load & Overwhelm
- **Is Week 1 truly manageable in 2-3 hours?** Be realistic.
- Are instructions chunked into digestible pieces?
- Is jargon explained in plain language, or assumed?
- Are there too many tasks visible at once?
- Would a reader finish Task 1 feeling accomplished or exhausted?

### 3. Clarity & Hand-Holding
- Are "click this exact button" instructions clear enough?
- Are screenshots or visual aids needed but missing?
- Would someone get stuck and not know what to do next?
- Are error scenarios explained? ("If you don't see X, try Y")
- Is help available if they get stuck?

### 4. Motivation & Buy-In
- **Does the "why" make sense?** (Why does hreflang matter?)
- Is the business impact clear and motivating?
- Are quick wins emphasized? (Small efforts, visible results)
- Is progress trackable? (Checkboxes, milestones)
- Does the team feel like heroes, or like they're fixing "your mess"?

### 5. Risk & Safety Concerns
- Are there hidden landmines? (Tasks that could break things)
- Is "save a backup" mentioned where needed?
- Are plugin installations safe and verified?
- Would a mistake cause panic or just need a simple fix?
- Is there a "test this safely" step before going live?

### 6. Time Estimates — Real or Optimistic?
For each task, honestly assess:
- Is 15 min realistic or will it take 45 min if they get stuck?
- Does "30 min" account for reading, thinking, checking?
- Are there dependencies that slow things down?
- Should time estimates be 2x higher for nervous first-timers?

### 7. Playbook vs Action Plan — Which to Show?
Compare the simplified PLAYBOOK to the full ACTION-PLAN:
- **Does the playbook hide too much?** (Will team feel blindsided later?)
- **Does the action plan show too much?** (Will 40 items cause panic?)
- **Should both be shared, or playbook only?**
- **Is there a middle ground?** (e.g., "Week 1 tasks now, full plan later")
- **Transparency vs overwhelm:** What's the right balance for this team?

### Task 5: Enable hreflang in WPML (15 min)

This is the single most important fix. Without hreflang, Google doesn't know your Finnish and English pages are connected.

1. Go to **WPML** > **Languages**
2. Find **SEO Options** section
3. Enable **"Use head langs"** (or "Add hreflang tags") — toggle ON
4. Set **x-default** to Finnish (your primary market)
5. Click **Save**

Then verify connections:
6. Go to **WPML** > **Translation Management**
7. Check that each English page is properly connected to its Finnish equivalent
8. Go to **WPML** > **Support** > **Troubleshooting** > click "Check translation status"

Finally check Yoast isn't blocking hreflang:
9. Go to **Yoast SEO** > **Settings** (or Search Appearance) > check there's no hreflang override

**Verify:** Visit `https://www.jarvisydan.com/en/` > right-click > View Page Source > Ctrl+F search for `hreflang`. You should see:
```html
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="fi" href="https://www.jarvisydan.com/" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en" href="https://www.jarvisydan.com/en/" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="x-default" href="https://www.jarvisydan.com/" />
```

> If hreflang still doesn't appear, check if the "WPML SEO" glue plugin needs to be installed. See WPML > Plugins.

- [ ] Done
