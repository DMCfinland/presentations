# Opus Review: Järvisydän SEO Playbook — Staff Handoff Quality Check

## Context

We're handing off a comprehensive SEO implementation playbook to a staff member at Järvisydän Oy (hotel & spa resort). The playbook guides them through WordPress admin tasks to improve the website's SEO score from 38/100 to 78/100 over 8 weeks.

**Target audience:** Staff member who:
- Has WordPress admin access
- Is comfortable with basic website editing
- May not be deeply technical
- Will be doing this alongside their regular job duties
- Needs to feel empowered, not overwhelmed

**Materials to review:**
1. WORDPRESS-PLAYBOOK.md (main handoff document — 16 tasks across 8 weeks)
2. Supporting guides (SCHEMA-DEPLOYMENT-GUIDE.md, HOTEL-SEO-BEST-PRACTICES.md, WP-ADMIN-IMPLEMENTATION-GUIDE.md)

## Your Mission

Review these materials as if you're the staff member receiving them. Evaluate:

### 1. Clarity & Accessibility
- Are instructions clear enough for someone who isn't deeply technical?
- Is jargon explained or avoided where possible?
- Are steps granular enough ("click this, type that") or too vague?
- Would a reader know exactly what to do, or get stuck?

### 2. Tone & Approachability
- Does it feel supportive and empowering, or demanding and technical?
- Is the tone appropriate for a colleague, not a contractor?
- Does it assume the reader is competent, or does it feel condescending?

### 3. Workload Assessment
- Are time estimates realistic? (Week 1: 2-3 hours, Week 2: 4-6 hours, etc.)
- Does the breakdown feel manageable alongside regular duties?
- Is the phased approach (quick wins → schema → content) logical?
- Would this feel like "I can do this" or "This is too much"?

### 4. Task Structure
- Are tasks well-sequenced? (Do later tasks depend on earlier ones properly?)
- Are checkboxes helpful for tracking progress?
- Are verification steps clear and actionable?

### 5. Support & Safety
- Does the reader know where to get help if stuck?
- Are "what if this doesn't work" scenarios addressed?
- Is there enough context about *why* each task matters?
- Are there warnings for anything that could break the site?

### 6. Gaps & Risks
- What's missing that would help the reader succeed?
- What could go wrong that isn't addressed?
- Are there tasks that actually need developer help but aren't flagged?
- Are plugin installations explained well enough?

## Output Format

Provide your review in this structure:

### Executive Summary (3-4 sentences)
Overall assessment: Is this handoff material ready, or does it need revision?

### Strengths (bullet list)
What works well that should be preserved.

### Concerns (bullet list, prioritized)
Issues that could cause confusion, anxiety, or failure.

### Specific Improvements Needed
For each concern, suggest concrete fixes:
- **Issue:** [what's wrong]
- **Impact:** [how it affects the staff member]
- **Fix:** [specific revision needed]

### Final Recommendation
- **Status:** Ready to hand off / Needs minor revisions / Needs significant rework
- **Confidence:** Your confidence the staff member will succeed with these materials (1-10)
- **Key message to Patrick:** One sentence of strategic advice
