## FOR 1658 HOLDINGS: AGM NOTICE NOTES

**Patrick as sole shareholder:**

1. **Notice technically still required** — even to yourself. Document that notice was "deemed given" or follow yhtiöjärjestys provisions.

2. **Timing is critical:**
   - Financial statements must be signed by April 30 (KPL 3:6 §)
   - AGM must be held by June 30 (OYL 5:3 §)
   - Allow 1-2 weeks for AGM after FS signing

3. **AGM sequence for holding structure:**
   - Subsidiary AGMs first (they decide dividends)
   - Holdings AGM last (receives dividends before its AGM)
   - Schedule all 10 AGMs in 2-3 week window (May-June)

4. **Simplified format OK** — sole shareholder doesn't need formal registration, proxy instructions, etc. But maintain structure for audit trail.

5. **Financial statements must be available 1 week before AGM** (OYL 5:21 §) — even if sole shareholder, this is best practice.

6. **Keep notice simple but complete** — you can condense but don't skip mandatory items.
