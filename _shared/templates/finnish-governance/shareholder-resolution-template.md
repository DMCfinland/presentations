## PÄÄTÖKSENTEON PERUSTE / BASIS FOR DECISION-MAKING

**Laillinen peruste / Legal basis:** OYL 5:4 §

_"Jos osakkeenomistaja yksin käyttää kaikkea äänivaltaa yhtiössä, hän voi päättää yhtiökokoukselle kuuluvista asioista. Päätökset on merkittävä pöytäkirjaan, joka on numeroitava, päivättävä ja allekirjoitettava."_

_"If a shareholder alone exercises all voting rights in the company, they may decide on matters belonging to the general meeting. Decisions must be recorded in minutes, which must be numbered, dated, and signed."_

**Menettely / Procedure:**

Ainoa osakkeenomistaja voi päättää yhtiökokoukselle kuuluvista asioista ilman varsinaista kokousta. Tämä päätös vastaa varsinaisen tai ylimääräisen yhtiökokouksen päätöstä.

_The sole shareholder may decide on matters belonging to the general meeting without an actual meeting. This decision is equivalent to a decision of an annual or extraordinary general meeting._

## FOR 1658 HOLDINGS: SOLE SHAREHOLDER DECISION NOTES

**Patrick as sole shareholder:**

1. **This is the fastest way to make shareholder decisions** — no need for formal meeting notice, attendance, etc.

2. **Still must be documented** — OYL 5:4 § requires numbered, dated, signed minutes. This is not optional.

3. **Can replace both AGM and EGM** — use this format for any shareholder-level decision.

4. **AGM deadline still applies** (June 30 for Dec 31 FY) — even when using sole shareholder decision format.

5. **Financial statements must be signed first** (by April 30) — you can't adopt unsigned FS.

6. **PRH filing still required** — sole shareholder decision equivalent to AGM must be filed with PRH within 2 months.

7. **Keep this simple** — don't overthink it. List the decisions, sign it, file it.

8. **Holding structure sequence:**
   - Subsidiary sole shareholder decisions first (adopt FS, decide dividends)
   - Holdings sole shareholder decision last (receives dividends, adopts Holdings FS)

9. **Use liberally** — for any shareholder decision between AGMs (articles changes, board elections, etc.)

10. **Minutes numbering:** These decisions count as shareholder meeting minutes. Number them in sequence (e.g., if AGM was meeting 1/2026, next sole shareholder decision is 2/2026).

## QUICK GUIDE: WHEN TO USE SOLE SHAREHOLDER DECISION

**✅ Use this format for:**
- Annual general meeting decisions (adopt FS, dividends, discharge, elections)
- Articles of association amendments
- Share capital increases/decreases
- Board member elections/removals
- Major contracts requiring shareholder approval
- Any other shareholder-level decision

**❌ DON'T use this for:**
- Board-level decisions (use board minutes or board resolution)
- Operational matters (board or MD authority)
- Routine management (MD authority)
