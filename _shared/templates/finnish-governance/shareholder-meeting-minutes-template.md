## FOR 1658 HOLDINGS: SOLE SHAREHOLDER NOTES

**Patrick as sole shareholder:**

1. **Minutes reviewer not required** (OYL 5:23 § 2 mom.) — sole shareholder signs alone with meeting chairman and secretary.

2. **AGM can be very brief** — sole shareholder can waive formalities like separate chairman election if practical (but recommend maintaining structure for audit trail).

3. **AGM deadline: June 30** (OYL 5:3 §) — for December 31 FY. Don't miss this.

4. **Notice requirement still applies** — even to yourself as sole shareholder. Document that notice was "deemed given" or follow yhtiöjärjestys provisions.

5. **Financial statements must be signed BEFORE AGM** (KPL 3:6 § deadline: April 30).

6. **Holding company AGM must come AFTER subsidiary AGMs** — dividend cascade requires subsidiaries to decide dividends first.

7. **PRH filing within 2 months of AGM** (OYL 8:10 §) — minutes extract + financial statements. Critical deadline.

8. **Discharge from liability is important** — even for yourself. It closes the book on the financial year and protects against future claims (unless fraud or gross negligence).
