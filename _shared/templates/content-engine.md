# Content Engine — Platform-Native Content Repurposing

Source: ECC content-engine skill, adapted for tourism/hospitality portfolio
Quality gate: `quality-gates/content-qg.md`

## When to Use
- Creating marketing content for DMC, Järvisydän, or Arctic Cruises
- Repurposing one asset (destination description, client case study) across platforms
- Building content calendars for social media
- Sales materials for B2B outreach

## Core Rules

1. **Adapt for the platform.** Never cross-post the same copy.
2. **Hooks matter more than summaries.** First line = first impression.
3. **One clear idea per post.** Don't bundle.
4. **Use specifics over slogans.** "138K€ Koli revenue in year 1" > "Amazing growth"
5. **Keep the ask small and clear.**

## First Questions

Before creating content, clarify:
- **Source asset:** What are we adapting? (destination description, tour itinerary, financial result, case study)
- **Audience:** Tour operators, direct travelers, investors, staff, partners?
- **Platforms:** LinkedIn, Instagram, newsletter, website, email, brochure?
- **Goal:** Awareness, booking conversion, B2B lead, recruitment, authority?

## Platform Guidance

### LinkedIn (B2B primary for DMC)
- Strong first line — statement or question
- Short paragraphs (2-3 lines max)
- Frame around lessons, results, and industry insights
- Tag relevant partners/clients (with permission)
- Best for: B2B tour operator outreach, industry positioning, team highlights

### Instagram (B2C for Järvisydän / Arctic Cruises)
- Visual-first — photo quality is everything
- Caption: hook → story → CTA
- Hashtag strategy: 5-10 targeted (not spam)
- Best for: Destination showcase, guest experiences, seasonal content

### Email / Newsletter
- Subject line = everything (A/B test when possible)
- One clear lens per email, not a bundle of updates
- Skimmable section headers
- Best for: Tour operator updates, seasonal rate cards, new product launches

### Website / Blog
- SEO-optimized headers and meta descriptions
- Destination descriptions: unique angle, not Wikipedia clone
- Internal linking to booking/contact pages
- Best for: Destination content, activity descriptions, company story

## Repurposing Flow

Default cascade from one source asset:

1. **Anchor asset:** Destination description, tour itinerary, case study, or financial milestone
2. **Extract 3-5 atomic ideas** from the anchor
3. **Write platform-native variants** for each target platform
4. **Trim repetition** across outputs
5. **Align CTAs** with platform intent (LinkedIn → "DM me", Instagram → "Link in bio", Email → "Reply to book")
