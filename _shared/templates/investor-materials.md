# Investor Materials — Consistency Framework

Source: ECC investor-materials skill, adapted for 1658 Holdings
Quality gate: `quality-gates/investor-materials-qg.md`

## When to Use
- Creating or revising pitch decks
- Writing investor memos or one-pagers
- Building financial models, milestone plans, use-of-funds tables
- DMC equity announcement for staff
- Any document where numbers must agree across materials

## The Golden Rule

**All investor materials must agree with each other.**

Create or confirm a single source of truth BEFORE writing:

## Asset Types

### Pitch Deck
See: `pitch-deck-flow.md` for the 12-slide framework.

### One-Pager / Memo
- State what the company does in one clean sentence
- Show why now (market timing, technology shift, regulatory change)
- Include traction and proof points early
- Make the ask precise (amount, instrument, timeline)
- Keep claims easy to verify

### Financial Model
- Explicit assumptions (not buried in formulas)
- Bear / base / bull cases
- Clean layer-by-layer revenue logic
- Milestone-linked spending (money unlocks capability, not just runway)
- Sensitivity analysis on the assumptions that matter most

### Equity / Staff Communication
- Lead with what's in it for them (not the mechanics)
- Explain the instrument simply (avoid jargon)
- Show how value grows with their contribution
- Address fears directly (dilution, tax, vesting cliff)
- Provide a clear timeline and next steps

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Unverifiable claims
- Fuzzy market sizing without assumptions
- Inconsistent team roles or titles across materials
- Revenue math that doesn't add up
- Inflated certainty where assumptions are fragile
- Different numbers in the deck vs the memo vs the model
