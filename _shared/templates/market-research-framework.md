# Market Research Framework — Decision-Oriented Research

Source: ECC market-research skill, adapted for portfolio company intelligence
Quality gate: `quality-gates/market-research-qg.md`

## When to Use
- Competitor analysis (DMC market, hospitality, tourism)
- Supplier evaluation (TravelTree alternatives, tech vendors)
- Market sizing (inbound tourism, lakeland potential)
- Investment due diligence (Koli/Marival, new acquisitions)
- Company intelligence (extending the 7-layer protocol)

## Core Principle

**Produce research that supports decisions, not research theater.**

Every research output must answer: "So what? What should Patrick do differently?"

## Research Standards

1. **Every important claim needs a source.** No unsourced numbers.
2. **Prefer recent data.** Call out stale data with the year.
3. **Include contrarian evidence.** What argues AGAINST the thesis?
4. **Translate to a decision.** End with a recommendation, not just a summary.
5. **Separate fact, inference, and recommendation** — label each clearly.

## Research Modes

### Competitor / Market Analysis
- Product reality, not marketing copy
- Pricing clues (public rate cards, Booking.com comparison, industry benchmarks)
- Strengths, weaknesses, and positioning gaps
- Distribution channels (direct vs OTA vs B2B)
- Relevant for: DMC competitor mapping, Järvisydän hospitality benchmarking

### Supplier / Vendor Evaluation
- How it works (integration complexity, API quality)
- Total cost (license + implementation + ongoing)
- Lock-in risk (data portability, contract terms)
- Alternatives evaluated (at least 2 comparisons)
- Relevant for: TravelTree, PMS systems, payment providers

### Investment Due Diligence
- Financial health (revenue trend, debt, profitability)
- Market position (share, growth, defensibility)
- Risk factors (regulatory, key person, concentration)
- Valuation sanity check (revenue multiple, comparable transactions)
- Relevant for: Koli analysis, M&A targets, portfolio health

### Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- Top-down from reports or public datasets
- Bottom-up from realistic acquisition assumptions
- Explicit assumptions for every leap in logic
- Cross-reference: does the bottom-up match the top-down within 2x?

## Output Structure

Every research deliverable follows this format:

```markdown
# [Research Title]

## Executive Summary (3-5 bullet points)

## Key Findings
### Finding 1: [Headline]
- Evidence: [data + source]
- Implication: [so what?]

### Finding 2: [Headline]
...

## Risks & Counterarguments
- [What argues against the thesis?]
- [What could go wrong?]

## Recommendation
[Specific action with rationale]

## Sources
[Numbered list, all cited in text]
```

## 1658 Holdings Context

When researching for portfolio companies:
- **GDPR rule:** Use generic search terms. Never include company names, client names, or financials in web searches unless Patrick explicitly requests it.
- **Cross-company lens:** Always check if findings apply to other portfolio companies, not just the requesting one.
- **Finnish context:** Include Finnish-language sources where relevant. Many market reports are Finnish-only.
- **Board-ready:** Research for Järvisydän or any board-level decision should be formatted for board presentation (executive summary first, details in appendix).
