# Quality Gate: Content / Marketing Materials

Run this checklist before publishing ANY marketing content.

## Platform Fit
- [ ] Each draft reads natively for its platform (not cross-posted copy)
- [ ] Hooks are strong and specific (not generic "Check this out!")
- [ ] CTA matches the platform intent (LinkedIn: DM, Instagram: link in bio, Email: reply)
- [ ] Tone matches the platform (LinkedIn: professional, Instagram: visual/casual, Email: personal)

## Content Quality
- [ ] One clear idea per post (not bundled)
- [ ] Specifics over slogans ("138K€ in year 1" > "Amazing growth")
- [ ] No hype language unless quoting someone
- [ ] Grammar and spelling checked (both EN and FI if bilingual)
- [ ] Brand voice is consistent with company positioning

## Tourism / Hospitality Specific
- [ ] Destination descriptions are unique (not Wikipedia clone)
- [ ] Photos referenced exist and are high-resolution
- [ ] Seasonal accuracy (don't promote aurora in July)
- [ ] Rate cards and pricing are current (check expiry dates)
- [ ] Supplier/partner mentioned has given permission for name use

## Legal / Compliance
- [ ] No client names without permission
- [ ] No guest photos without consent
- [ ] Claims are factual (no "best in Finland" without qualification)
- [ ] Finnish Consumer Protection Act compliance (if pricing shown)
- [ ] GDPR: no personal data in marketing materials without consent
