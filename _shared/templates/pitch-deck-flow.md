# Pitch Deck Flow — 12-Slide Framework

Source: ECC investor-materials skill, adapted for 1658 Holdings
Quality gate: `quality-gates/investor-materials-qg.md`

## When to Use
- Investment pitches (DMC equity, new ventures)
- Company presentations to partners, banks, or boards
- Accelerator/grant applications with slide requirements

## The Golden Rule

**All investor materials must agree with each other.**

Before drafting any slide, confirm a single source of truth for:
- Traction metrics (revenue, clients, growth rate)
- Pricing and revenue assumptions
- Raise size and instrument (equity %, convertible, grant)
- Use of funds breakdown
- Team bios and titles
- Milestones and timelines

If conflicting numbers appear — STOP and resolve before drafting.

## 12-Slide Flow

| # | Slide | Content | Time |
|---|-------|---------|------|
| 1 | **Company + Wedge** | One sentence: what you do and why it matters now | 15s |
| 2 | **Problem** | The pain point. Use data or a client quote. | 30s |
| 3 | **Solution** | How you solve it. Keep it concrete, not abstract. | 30s |
| 4 | **Product / Demo** | Screenshots, workflow, or service walkthrough | 45s |
| 5 | **Market** | TAM/SAM/SOM with explicit assumptions. Bottom-up sanity check. | 30s |
| 6 | **Business Model** | Revenue streams, pricing, margins, commission structure | 30s |
| 7 | **Traction** | Revenue history, client count, growth rate, key wins | 30s |
| 8 | **Team** | Key people, relevant experience, why THIS team | 20s |
| 9 | **Competition** | Positioning map or comparison table. Honest. | 20s |
| 10 | **The Ask** | What you want: amount, instrument, timeline | 15s |
| 11 | **Use of Funds + Milestones** | Where the money goes. What it unlocks. | 20s |
| 12 | **Appendix** | Financials, tech stack, reference clients, legal structure | — |

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Unverifiable claims ("we'll be the #1 in 2 years")
- Fuzzy market sizing without bottom-up check
- Inconsistent team roles across materials
- Revenue math that doesn't add up
- Hype language ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "disruptive")
- Missing the ask — always be specific about what you want
