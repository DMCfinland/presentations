"40% cheaper than Rhine" is mathematically false for 2027. Rhine 7-night net rates to operators sit at €2,800–€4,000+ pp (per diem €400–€570); Saimaa's €1,920 net for 7 nights is not 40% cheaper once you factor in the shore-accommodation model and shorter water time. PMs will spot the apples-to-oranges trick instantly.
**Fix:** Remove the percentage claim or replace it with a footnote table showing exact comparable net packages from AmaWaterways/Viking 2027 brochures.

"Better beds than Göta" is marketing spin that will backfire. Göta operators know their guests accept tiny cabins for the unique canal experience; your shore-based 5★ beds are irrelevant to the comparison and sound desperate.
**Fix:** Delete the claim entirely. Replace with "land-based 5★ resort nights vs Göta's compact onboard berths" and let the operator decide.

"First sauna on a European inland cruise vessel" is provably false. **Fix:** Change to "Finland's first scheduled lake cruise with a purpose-built floating sauna lounge on the vessel" and attach the exact refit drawings and certification timeline.
