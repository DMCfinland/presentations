### **Structural Audit: Investment Product Deck Workflow**

### **1. COMPLETENESS**

**Score: 4/10**

**Justification:** The workflow is exceptionally complete for the *technical build process* of the specified deliverables. It covers asset creation from planning to final file output. However, it is critically incomplete as a *business process for sales*. It operates in a vacuum, ignoring the essential steps that precede the decision to build a deck and the crucial activities that follow its delivery. It details how to build the arrow but not how to aim it or confirm it hit the target.

**Top 3 Improvements:**
1.  **Add Pre-Qualification Step (Phase -1):** A custom deck, even one built in 60 minutes, is too expensive for an unqualified prospect. A preliminary step is needed to confirm the prospect's suitability (e.g., accredited investor status, stated interest, capacity). This could be a simple checklist or a call brief.
2.  **Integrate Competitor Analysis:** The AI-driven "best practices" research is a good start, but it's not a substitute for direct competitor analysis. Phase 0 or 1 must include a step to identify the 1-2 primary alternatives the prospect is likely considering and ensure the deck's narrative directly counters or outperforms them.
3.  **Add Post-Delivery Protocol (Phase 4):** The workflow ends at delivery. A sales process does not. A "Phase 4: Follow-Up & Feedback Loop" is essential. This should include protocols for:
    *   Tracking opens/views (if possible).
    *   Scheduled follow-up call/email cadence.
    *   A structured method for capturing prospect objections and feedback to refine the master deck template.

### **2. SEQUENCING**

**Score: 5/10**

**Justification:** The internal sequence of the build (Photos → HTML → Derivatives) is logical. However, the overall process sequence has critical flaws that introduce significant business and compliance risk. It places strategic validation (Kill Vectors) and research *after* the plan is supposedly locked, and it completely omits a legal review gate at the correct stage.

**Top 3 Improvements:**
1.  **Move Kill Vector Analysis into Phase 0:** Kill Vector analysis is a strategic planning input, not a research task. It should be performed *as part of* the planning phase to inform the core structure and messaging, not as a parallel task while other research is happening. The answers to "What will skeptics attack?" should fundamentally shape the `GOAL` and `PRODUCT` definitions.
2.  **Mandate Financial Model Sign-off in Phase 0:** The current workflow validates the *presentation* of the financial model, not the model itself. The underlying spreadsheet (the "source of truth") with all assumptions, depreciation schedules, and revenue forecasts must be finalized and signed off by a partner or financial controller *before* Phase 0 is considered complete. This was a critical failure in the Sunseeker project and is not adequately addressed here.
3.  **Insert a Mandatory Legal & Compliance Gate:** There is no mention of legal review. For any investment product, this is a fatal omission. A "Legal & Compliance Review" step must be inserted after Phase 2 (Build) and before Phase 3 (Delivery). This gate ensures all claims are defensible, disclaimers are adequate, and the offer complies with relevant securities regulations in the target jurisdiction.

### **5. TIME/COST EFFICIENCY**

**Score: 7/10**

**Justification:** The workflow is clearly designed to combat the primary time-waster: rework from pivots. The proposed 45-60 minute timeline is, however, highly optimistic. Reviewing AI outputs, writing compelling HNWI-level copy (which AI struggles with), and performing manual checks will realistically take longer. A 90-120 minute cycle time is more credible. The major efficiency gain is not in the build speed itself, but in the dramatic reduction of wasted cycles from 10 sessions down to a potential 2-3.

**Top 3 Improvements:**
1.  **Set a Realistic Time Budget:** Adjust the stated time to **90-120 minutes** for a subsequent run. This manages expectations and prevents rushing, which leads to errors that ultimately cost more time. The value proposition is "single-session completion," not "under-an-hour completion."
2.  **Create Pre-Approved Content Libraries:** The biggest time-sink during the build phase is writing copy. Create a library of pre-approved, legally-vetted text blocks for common elements: standard disclaimers, team bios, company history, scheduling mechanism descriptions, exit clauses. This turns writing into assembly.
3.  **Front-load All Asset Gathering:** The workflow implies photos are gathered in Phase 2. All required assets (financial model, photos, legal disclaimers, team bios) should be gathered and signed off *before* Phase 2 begins. The build phase should be pure assembly and rendering, not a scavenger hunt.

### **REVISED WORKFLOW SECTIONS**

Based on the audit, here are the recommended revisions to key sections of your workflow.

#### **REVISED: Phase 0 — STRATEGIC LOCK**

Run in lock mode. A "Yes" to all questions is required to exit this phase. Any subsequent change to a locked parameter forces a workflow reset.

1.  **QUALIFICATION:** Is the prospect qualified? (Profile matches, capacity confirmed, intent expressed).
2.  **GOAL:** What is the single decision this package must drive? (e.g., "Schedule a call to review financial model," "Commit to a site visit").
3.  **AUDIENCE:** Who is the specific recipient? (e.g., "Finnish family office, principal is an engineer, numbers-first, risk-averse").
4.  **PRODUCT:** Is the asset, investment structure, pricing, and all financial model inputs finalized and signed off by [Partner/Finance]?
5.  **COMPETITION:** What are the 1-2 likely alternatives they are considering? How does our narrative specifically address them?
6.  **TONE:** Aspiration-first or numbers-first? (Confirm based on Audience profile).
7.  **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Which deliverables are required for this specific sales cadence? (e.g., "HTML for initial outreach, PDF for follow-up").
8.  **KILL VECTORS:** What are the top 3-5 objections or deal-killers for this specific audience and asset? (These must be explicitly addressed in the content plan).
9.  **LEGAL:** Has legal counsel provided the required disclaimer language and reviewed the offering structure?

**Phase 0 Output:** A signed-off "Session Brief" containing all 9 locked parameters. This document is the source of truth for the build.
