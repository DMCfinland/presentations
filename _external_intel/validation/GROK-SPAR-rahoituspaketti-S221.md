Gap report: Peer-review of Finnish real estate financing package (423k EUR tourism land plots in Rantasalmi)

Q1: SANEERAUS RISK
[verdict] High residual risk; G6 confirmation mitigates but does not legally eliminate YSL 27§ termination exposure. Finnish banks treat distressed-lessee cash-flow loans as elevated risk and typically require extra mitigants or view as delay/rejection trigger (Harper + Lucas).

[Harper findings] YSL 47/1993 §27 (updated 23.3.2023/479) expressly allows the debtor-lessee (Lomakylä Järvisydän Oy) to terminate any lease with 2 months’ notice after saneeraus proceedings begin, overriding contract terms. Compensation is limited to necessary repossession costs + reasonable damages. Pesänhoitaja’s G6 confirmation that the lease is mandatory for business continuity in the approved program demonstrates current intent to maintain it, but is administrative only — it does not constitute a binding waiver or court order extinguishing the statutory right if the program is later amended, fails, or converts to bankruptcy (Harper). No direct case law binds banks to G6 alone; KKO precedents (2022:74 and related 2025 interpretations) confirm terminations and resulting claims remain possible (Harper).

[Benjamin findings] Numbers verified independently: annual annuity debt service ≈ 37,547 EUR (principal 423k @4% 15yr); DSCR 120k / 37,547 ≈ 3.20x and breakeven rent drop before 1.20x DSCR is 62% (Benjamin). This quantitative strength does not offset qualitative saneeraus risk flagged by others (Benjamin).

[Lucas challenges] Conservative credit committee rejects or heavily delays: (1) single distressed tenant = core cash-flow vulnerability in low-liquidity rural tourism plots; (2) YSL 27§ right persists despite G6; (3) no evidence of post-restructuring lessee financials or backup re-leasing plan. Banks routinely demand rent escrow, lease assignment to lender, tripartite agreement with pesänhoitaja, or owner-level guarantees on the lease (Lucas).

[recommendation] Obtain written pesänhoitaja undertaking (or court confirmation) that the lease cannot be terminated without lender consent + rent deposit/escrow or assignment. Prepare fallback re-leasing analysis. Expect bank to classify as “special mention” until resolved (all agents).

Q2: PROPERTY IDENTIFIER ISSUE
[verdict] Clear documentation gap; unresolved identifier mismatch will cause immediate registration failure and application delay/rejection until fixed (Harper + Lucas).

[Harper findings] MML (Maanmittauslaitos) requires the current kiinteistötunnus in all loan, mortgage, and lien documents. Old code 681-418-1-65 was consolidated/merged into new 681-418-1-169 on 23.6.2022; banks demand explicit proof of continuity via MML extract/ote showing the merger history (Harper).

[Benjamin findings] No numerical input here, but cross-checks confirm only current MML register identifier is acceptable for collateral perfection (Benjamin).

[Lucas challenges] This is a title defect risk: mismatched ID prevents valid mortgage registration. Bank will reject until official MML documentation links old → new and confirms clean chain of title (Lucas).

[recommendation] Submit: (1) current MML lainhuutotodistus and rasitustodistus under new identifier 681-418-1-169 only; (2) separate MML historical extract proving the 23.6.2022 consolidation/merger; (3) updated application with new ID exclusively. Do not use old code anywhere (Harper + Lucas).

Q3: APPLICANT STRUCTURE
[verdict] Both structures have material weaknesses; banks accept either but scrutinise (B) startup heavily and prefer documented business rationale + strong personal guarantees. Neither is clearly superior without additional mitigants (Harper + Lucas).

[Harper findings] For commercial/tourism investment loans, Finnish banks often favour limited-liability company borrower (Finland DMC Oy) for ring-fencing and corporate tax treatment (20% CIT, full interest deductibility). Private individuals trigger capital-income taxation (30/34%) on rental income with more limited deductions post-2010s rules. Both require omavelkainen takaus here (Harper).

[Benjamin findings] No numerical disagreement; DSCR/LTV calculations identical regardless of borrower entity (Benjamin).

[Lucas challenges] (A) Private individuals: unlimited personal recourse exposes applicants’ entire wealth and may constrain future borrowing; rural tourism asset volatility amplifies personal risk. (B) Startup Oy (y-tunnus 3219901-8, no operating history): higher perceived credit risk, demands deeper business-plan review and possibly more equity injection. Banks view either as acceptable only if personal guarantees are iron-clad and cash-flow is de-risked (Lucas).

[recommendation] Recommend structure (B) company borrower + full personal guarantees (standard market practice for investment loans). Provide: 3-year financial projections for Oy, owner tax returns, and explicit tax opinion on interest deductibility vs private capital-income treatment. Flag to bank which structure is chosen (Harper + Lucas).

Q4: CROSS-COLLATERAL DETAILS
[verdict] “Total value listing” is insufficient; banks require granular, fresh per-property documentation or the collateral will not be accepted at stated LTV (all agents).

[Harper findings] Standard Finnish bank practice demands individual current lainhuutotodistus + rasitustodistus for every cross-collateral property, plus exact identifiers, ownership confirmation by applicants, and priority ranking. Aggregate EUR 1.387M figure alone is rejected (Harper).

[Benjamin findings] LTV 22% verified mathematically but contingent on fully documented collateral value (Benjamin).

[Lucas challenges] Missing per-property docs = perfection risk. Committee will reject or haircut collateral value until every property has: fresh (<1-3 months) todistukset, independent valuation reports, mortgage pledge setup if needed, and proof applicants own them free of prior liens (Lucas).

[recommendation] Deliver for each cross-collateral property: (1) fresh lainhuutotodistus, (2) fresh rasitustodistus, (3) independent valuation, (4) exact kiinteistötunnus matching application. Prepare mortgage applications/pledges simultaneously (Harper + Lucas).
