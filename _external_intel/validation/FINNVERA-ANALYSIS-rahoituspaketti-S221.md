# Finnvera Financing Analysis — Rantasalmi Land Plot Acquisition

**Case:** 405,000 € tourism land purchase (two plots), leased to Lomakylä Järvisydän Oy at 120,000 €/yr / 30-year lease. Lessee in yrityssaneeraus. Buyers: Patrick & Sebastian Heiskanen (private individuals or via Finland DMC Oy).

---

## 1. Applicable Finnvera Products

### A. Investointilaina (Investment Loan)
| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Purpose | Fixed asset investments: machinery, equipment, real property, construction |
| Loan size | 50,000 – 8,000,000 € (SME range) |
| Max Finnvera share | Up to 75% of investment cost; typically used alongside bank financing |
| Interest rate | Variable (Euribor 3/6/12M) + margin ~2.0–3.5%; fixed rate option available |
| Repayment | 3–15 years, grace period possible |
| Collateral | Typically mortgage on financed property + personal guarantees |
| Eligibility | Companies registered in Finland; SME criteria apply |

**Assessment for this case:** The land plots are fixed assets leased for commercial tourism use — this fits the investment loan framing IF the applicant is a company (Finland DMC Oy). The 30-year lease generating 120k€/yr is a strong cash flow argument. **Key risk: Finnvera typically requires the borrowing entity to operate a business, not merely hold and lease property.**

---

### B. Yrityslaina (Business Loan)
| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Purpose | Growth investments, working capital, acquisitions |
| Loan size | 10,000 – 1,000,000 € (often co-financed with bank) |
| Max Finnvera share | Up to 50% of total financing need |
| Interest rate | ~3.0–5.0% depending on risk profile |
| Repayment | 3–10 years |
| Eligibility | Companies with operating history; growth intent required |

**Assessment:** Less suited here — 423k€ total need would push this to Investointilaina territory. Yrityslaina is more for operational/growth capital than land acquisition.

---

### C. Finnvera Takaus (Guarantee)
| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Purpose | Bank loan guarantee — Finnvera covers 50–80% of bank loan default risk |
| How it works | Your bank provides the loan; Finnvera guarantees it → enables bank to lend more/at better terms |
| Fee | 0.75–2.5%/year on guaranteed portion |
| Eligibility | Companies; banks propose guarantee on behalf of borrower |

**Assessment:** This is arguably the **most practical route** for this case. Finland DMC Oy approaches its bank for a 350–400k€ property loan; the bank applies for Finnvera guarantee. Reduces bank's risk → higher approval probability, potentially better rate. This is the standard Finnish SME property financing structure.

---

### D. Kasvulaina (Growth Loan)
| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Purpose | Significant growth investments, internationalization |
| Loan size | 150,000 – 1,000,000 € |
| Requires | ELY Centre or Business Finland funding alongside |
| Eligibility | Companies with demonstrable growth ambition; often tech/export focus |

**Assessment:** Weak fit. Kasvulaina requires co-funding from another public source and is growth/export oriented. A domestic land lease play does not typically qualify.

---

### E. Business Finland — Tourism Programs
Business Finland runs periodic tourism development programs (e.g., Sustainable Travel Finland, rural tourism investments). However, these are **grants/development subsidies** for tourism operators, not property acquisition financing. The lessee (Järvisydän) might qualify, but the buyer/lessor (DMC Oy / private individuals) generally would not for this purpose.

---

## 2. Private Individuals vs. Company — Critical Point

**Finnvera does NOT finance private individuals for investment/property purchases.** All Finnvera products require a registered Finnish company as the borrower. This is a hard eligibility gate.

**Implication:** The acquisition must be structured through **Finland DMC Oy** (or a new holding SPV) to access Finnvera. Patrick and Sebastian as private individuals are ineligible directly.

---

## 3. The Yrityssaneeraus Problem

This is the most significant risk factor for Finnvera financing:

- The lessee (Lomakylä Järvisydän Oy) is in **yrityssaneeraus** (court-supervised restructuring under Finnish law).
- Finnvera's credit risk assessment will view a yrityssaneeraus counterparty as high-risk income source.
- The 120,000 €/yr lease income cannot be treated as secure cash flow during saneeraus — the administrator could challenge or renegotiate lease terms.
- Finnvera explicitly avoids financing where the primary repayment source is from a company in insolvency proceedings.

**This is likely a deal-breaker for Finnvera direct financing.** A bank guarantee (Takaus) is slightly more survivable since the guarantee fee is paid upfront and the bank holds the primary credit view — but the bank itself will have the same concern.

---

## 4. Real Estate / Land — Finnvera's General Stance

Finnvera's mandate is to promote Finnish SME growth and exports. Pure kiinteistösijoitus (real estate investment) as a business model receives **low priority** — Finnvera is not designed to replace commercial real estate lenders.

However, land directly tied to an operating tourism business (hospitality infrastructure) is treated differently than speculative real estate. The 30-year lease to an active tourism operator strengthens the case. The framing must be: "tourism infrastructure enabling commercial operations" — not "property investment."

## 6. Recommendation

### CONDITIONAL — with low probability in current form

**Verdict: Do not lead with Finnvera as primary financing source given the yrityssaneeraus situation. Explore as guarantee mechanism only, and only after saneeraus resolution.**

**Rationale:**
- The yrityssaneeraus of the lessee makes Finnvera approval improbable. Finnvera's credit committee will reject a deal where repayment depends on lease income from a company in insolvency proceedings.
- If saneeraus concludes successfully (vahvistettu saneerausohjelma) before application, the picture changes materially — a completed restructuring with confirmed payment schedule is bankable.
- The strongest viable path today is **commercial bank financing** (OP Yrityspankki, Nordea, Aktia) secured by mortgage on the plots + personal guarantees from Patrick & Sebastian. Plots at 405k€ with 120k€/yr lease (29% yield) are strong collateral.
- Finnvera Takaus as a **bank guarantee add-on** is worth exploring once a bank expresses conditional interest — it can unlock better terms or fill a collateral gap.

## 7. Process Steps (If Pursuing Finnvera)

1. **Resolve ownership structure** — acquire via Finland DMC Oy (or new SPV). Private individuals cannot apply.
2. **Wait for saneeraus status clarity** — apply only after saneerausohjelma is confirmed or lessee exits restructuring.
3. **Bank-first approach** — present the deal to 2–3 banks. Ask each: "Would a Finnvera guarantee strengthen this application?" Banks initiate Finnvera guarantees on behalf of borrowers.
4. **Finnvera direct application (if applicable)** — apply via finnvera.fi/asiakaspalvelu or local Finnvera office (Mikkeli/Savonlinna serves South Savo). Application requires: financial statements of DMC Oy, investment plan, lease agreement, land valuation.
5. **Timeline** — Finnvera processes SME loan applications in 2–6 weeks for straightforward cases. Complex cases (restructuring counterparty) may take 8–12 weeks or be referred back.

---

**Bottom line:** Finnvera is not the right primary instrument today due to the yrityssaneeraus of Järvisydän. The deal's underlying economics are sound (29% gross yield, 30-year lease, tourism land). Structure through DMC Oy, secure commercial bank financing first, and revisit Finnvera Takaus once the lessee's restructuring is resolved.
