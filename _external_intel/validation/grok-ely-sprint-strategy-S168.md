# Grok Spar — ELY + Sprint Grant Strategy

## VERDICT
"The entire strategy rests on the delusional belief that a 4-year-old 5-person tourism DMC can treat public grants as a predictable cash machine for operational marketing and vague 'feasibility' while ignoring every eligibility, cumulation, and timing rule."

Critical flaw that collapses first: **Project A (FAM) total ineligibility + illegal own-financing claim.**

---

## Finding 1 — ELY OMARAHOITUS: FATAL ❌

Lappeenranta's €20k city grant **cannot** count as own financing. It is municipal public support — de minimis aid. ELY requires genuine private contribution (own cash, own labor at real cost, private equity).

Under EU Reg 2023/2831 Art. 5 (cumulation rules): public grants cannot be stacked on identical eligible costs beyond aid intensity ceiling (typically 50% for ELY kehittämisavustus). The "own financing = Lappeenranta €20k + management time" is **not valid**. Application collapses on basic eligibility screening.

**What counts as valid own financing:** Private cash, owner labor at documented cost, private debt/equity. NOT: city grants, VisitFinland support, other public aid.

---

## Finding 2 — FAM TRIP COSTS: NOT ELY-ELIGIBLE ❌

ELY development grants target genuine business development (product innovation, process improvements, new internationalization planning). FAM trip costs — hosting operators, outreach materials, conversion tracking — are **textbook marketing and operational sales activities**.

ELY official guidance explicitly excludes "operatiivisen toiminnan tai markkinoinnin kuluihin" (operational or marketing expenses). Relabeling as "export plan" won't survive scrutiny. Project A as structured = ineligible.

---

## Finding 3 — BF SPRINT R&D GATE: FAILS ❌

Pure feasibility study (market research + technical architecture spec) does **not** satisfy ≥2/3 R&D requirement. BF Sprint R&D per Finnish R&D Decree 1444/2014 = solution development, prototype/MVP implementation, technical feasibility verification, customer piloting with feedback.

Market research + architecture specs + business planning are explicitly non-R&D add-ons. Application gets reclassified or zeroed. **Sprint requires actual code/prototype work.**
