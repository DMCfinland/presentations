## 1. EU Package Travel Directive 2015/2302 — MAJOR GAPS

**Pre-contract halt items:**
- No organiser vs. retailer role clarity (Arctic Cruises Oy = vessel operator; Finland DMC Oy = DMC partner). PTD Art. 3/4 requires explicit organiser liability assignment.
- Missing insolvency protection disclosure (ATOL-equivalent for Finnish/EU sales)
- Missing: transfer of contract rights, complaint handling, price revision rules (max 8% trigger)
- UEC (unavoidable & extraordinary circumstances) handling not defined
- Wheelchair access = [TBD] — must confirm before PTD-compliant marketing
- Revised PTD (Dec 2025 political agreement, Mar 2026 Parliament adoption, ~May 2026 entry into force, phased to 2029 application) — 2027 operations under transitional rules

**Chain liability risk:** Document states "All downstream materials derived from this" — if master lacks organiser clarity, every derivative deck/website/FAM invite inherits the gap.
