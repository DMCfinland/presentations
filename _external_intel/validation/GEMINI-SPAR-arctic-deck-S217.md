This damages the perception of exclusivity. For B2B, "exclusive" implies a small, highly-curated group (e.g., 10-15 operators). Announcing a FAM trip for 80 operators signals a mass-market approach, not a premium, selective partnership. It undermines any claim of scarcity or special status.

*   **Recommendation:** Change the copy to "Join our inaugural FAM trip for key partners." Focus on the quality of the partnership, not a falsely exclusive number.

**3. Naming Local/Regional Partners**

*   **Impact on Credibility:** Naming **Go Saimaa**, **Järvisydän (with Green Key logo)**, and **Sahanlahti Resort** adds immense credibility. It shows local buy-in, verifies sustainability claims (Green Key is a powerful signal in DACH), and demonstrates a robust operational network.
