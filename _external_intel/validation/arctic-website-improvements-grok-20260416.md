Challenges to our competitive framing
Our hypothesis (“PONANT’s price-conscious buyer (€8,000+ Antarctic aspirant) is our primary addressable segment”) is materially inaccurate for this product.

PONANT = expedition/adventure polar luxury (remote oceans, younger skew, French/Euro adventure crowd, high-price extremes).
Lake Saimaa = accessible inland Finnish lake wilderness, serene nature/culture/wellness, UNESCO geopark, 7 nights, short-haul from DACH. Aligns far more with booming European river/lake cruise segment (Viking/AmaWaterways-style) popular with 50-68yo DACH seniors seeking Nordic serenity without polar logistics/risk.
DACH river/lake demand is exploding (37% market share, 8-9% CAGR); Finland nature tourism already promoted to Germans/Austrians for purity/serenity. Not “down-market Antarctic aspirants.”
Price anchoring to €8k+ competitors can help value perception but risks “cheap” or “not prestigious enough” signal in true luxury circles. Better frame as exclusive small-ship Nordic experience on its own merits. [Lucas][Harper]

Lucas’s devil’s advocate
We’re wrong on priority and framing. Ranking cosmetics/scarcity high before fixing the actual funnel (broken form + multi-step discovery) is classic shiny-object syndrome. Pre-launch static site: 80% of conversion impact comes from technical reliability + lead capture, not badges or icons. Scarcity #2 is actively dangerous (fake urgency erodes the exact trust luxury demands). B2B on B2C homepage dilutes focus. PONANT comparison is aspirational over-reach that ignores DACH river-cruise reality and risks positioning us as the “budget option.” Missed multi-lang + waitlist + video would 3-5x the impact of half this list. We’re optimizing the wrong things first. [Lucas]

Verdict: CONDITIONAL SHIP
Ship the technical/core funnel fixes (1,7,6,4) immediately — they are high-certainty wins. Rework #2 scarcity (drop misleading “High Demand” or qualify heavily) and #3 (make B2B secondary). Add the two biggest missed gaps (multi-lang + waitlist) before launch. Polish #5/#8 can follow. Do not ship #2 as written. Overall direction is sound but execution must prioritize trust, funnel, and DACH reality over competitor cosplay. This gets us to strong pre-launch lead flow without ethical or perceptual risk.
