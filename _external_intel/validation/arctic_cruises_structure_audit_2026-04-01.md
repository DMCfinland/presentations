# Gemini 2.5 Pro — Arctic Cruises FAM2026 Structure Audit
**Date:** 2026-04-01 | **Model:** gemini-2.5-pro | **Session:** S139

## Verdict
Document order (programme → 1-pager → brochure) is CORRECT. But 2 precursor documents missing.

## Critical Gaps Found

### 1. Missing: Participant Profile (before programme build)
- "International tour operators" too vague — DMOs will immediately ask: which markets? price point?
- Build 1-page ideal participant profile: markets (DACH/Benelux/UK), specialisation (premium FIT, sustainable), business potential
- This becomes part of the DMO pitch

### 2. Missing: Master Budget + Funding Matrix (before DMO approach)
- Need line-item budget at 40 pax vs 80 pax
- Include: HEL-LPR coaches, Olavinlinna entrance, staff costs, 15% contingency
- Parallel funding matrix: who covers which line

### 3. Financing gaps before DMO approach
- Variable 40–80 pax = variable cost = DMO says "come back with a number" → present a CAP (e.g. max 60)
- Logistics HEL↔LPR is a 5-figure cost — get binding coach quote BEFORE DMO meeting
- Finland DMC Oy has no skin in the game → add cash/in-kind contribution to budget

### 4. Programme timetable risk
- Imatra stop: 80 people off/on ship = 45-50 min minimum. Schedule likely underestimates.
- One 30-min delay Day 1 cascades into ruined Sahanlahti arrival/dinner
- Fix: add 30-min buffer to every stop. Cut activity if schedule doesn't fit.

### 5. 1-pager strongest argument
"Your investment covers accommodation for [X] curated senior product managers from premium operators in DACH/Benelux/UK — ready to put this itinerary in their 2027 catalogues."
→ Frame as product development, not marketing. Multi-region coalition, not charity.

### 6. Brochure must-haves for DMO buy-in
- Bold vision headline + business case (not just "beautiful trip")
- Partner logos prominently (social proof, FOMO)
- Participant profile: "invitation-only, 40–80 senior product managers from premium operators"
- Visual route map (better than text)

### 7. Brochure must-haves for VisitFinland forwarding
- Visit Finland strategy language: "sustainable slow travel", "high-yield tourism", "bookable product", "extending summer season"
- Frame as: launching a replicable PRODUCT, not a destination event
- World-class design — must look already well-funded
