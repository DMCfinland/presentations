## Top 3 Narrative Risks

**Risk 1 [Lucas] — "0€ lisäkustannus" claim — HARDEST ASSUMPTION / most credibility-damaging**
Fixed €84k total likely excludes heavy variable opex during personal cruising. 64ft motor yacht: 200–400L/hr diesel = €300–800+/hr fuel alone; plus provisioning, port fees, crew overtime. Buyer who cruises full 4 weeks will see €2k–5k+/week extra invoices. This is the single most credibility-damaging claim on audit.

**Risk 2 [Harper][Lucas] — Charter income scenarios create legal risk even when labelled**
Even labelled "conservative/realistic/optimistic" can be read as implied forecast in EU/FI sales decks. Buyers sue if income < €1,400 (market seasonality, downtime, utilization risk). Must carry bold disclaimer: "Projections hypothetical; past performance not indicative; no guarantee."

**Risk 3 [Benjamin][Lucas] — H1/H2 split + cash-reserve silence**
25-30% / 70-75% means owners must fund ~€2k–3k/share reserve in low season or face special assessment. Omitting this kills trust when cash call arrives.
