Day-by-Day Programme

01 Sunday 31 August 2026 Lappeenranta
  • All day Arrivals — Helsinki → Lappeenranta by InterCity train (recommended, approx. 2h 30min). We suggest arriving by early afternoon.
  • 14:00 Embarkation at Lappeenranta harbour. Welcome reception on board M/S Carelia. Meet the team: Saku Hyttinen (Founder), Laura Ilvonen (Trade Relations) and the Captain.
  • 16:00 Departure. First passage through the Saimaa southern archipelago — 4,400 islands, UNESCO Global Geopark. Briefing on Saimaa ecology and seal habitats from the Captain.
  • Evening Dinner on board — regional fish, foraged ingredients, Finnish kitchen. Overnight: Sahanlahti Resort, Puumala.
02 Monday 1 September 2026 Järvisydän
  • Morning Breakfast on board as Carelia cruises through the archipelago. Possible natural observation of the Saimaa ringed seal — approximately 500 individuals exist worldwide.
  • 11:00 Olavinlinna medieval castle passage — Savonlinna. One of Finland's most iconic views, seen from the water as the castle was designed to be approached.
  • Afternoon Arrive Heinävesi area. Check in at Järvisydän Resort (Green Key certified, European Region of Gastronomy 2024). Time to explore the grounds.
  • 19:00 Gala dinner on the lakeside terrace — local producers, Finnish gastronomy. The sun sets after 21:00 in late August; the sky turns orange above the water.
  • Evening Sauna session at Järvisydän. Overnight: Järvisydän Resort.
03 Tuesday 2 September 2026 On board
  • Morning Breakfast at Järvisydän. Free time — nature walk, kayaking, or quiet time on the lake shore.
  • 10:30 Re-embark M/S Carelia. Departure from Järvisydän.
  • 13:00 Commercial briefing session on board — net rates, booking process, 2027 season departures. Commercial agreements available for signature. Early-partner commission: 20% for operators who confirm by 15 July 2026.
  • 15:00 FinnConcierge product demonstration — multilingual AI and human assistant platform (DE / EN / FR / FI). Live demo of client-facing features.
  • Evening Smoke sauna at Sahanlahti Resort. Farewell dinner. Overnight: Sahanlahti Resort.
04 Wednesday 3 September 2026 Return
  • Morning Breakfast at Sahanlahti Resort.
  • 09:00 Return cruise: Sahanlahti → Lappeenranta. Final passage through the archipelago.
  • 12:00 Arrival Lappeenranta harbour. Disembarkation.
  • 12:00– Full digital media pack distributed — 50+ high-resolution photographs with commercial usage rights. Commercial agreements finalized. Departures: Lappeenranta → Helsinki by train.

Getting to Lappeenranta

  • By train: Helsinki → Lappeenranta, InterCity direct (~2h 30min, recommended). Trains run regularly throughout the day.
  • By air: Direct flights to Helsinki (HEL) from Frankfurt, Munich, Vienna, London, Amsterdam, Stockholm. 45-min onward flight to Lappeenranta (LPR) also available.
  • Recommend arriving: Sunday 31 August, morning or midday. Embarkation is 14:00.
  • Departures: Wednesday 3 September from noon. Return to Helsinki by early afternoon.

What to bring

  • Smart casual clothing — no formal requirement on board or at resorts
  • Fleece or light down jacket (September evenings: 12–16°C on the water)
  • Light waterproof layer
  • Swimwear and towel (smoke sauna both evenings)
  • Binoculars — recommended for possible seal observation and archipelago wildlife
  • Camera — media rights: unlimited commercial use for promoting Arctic Cruises to your clients
