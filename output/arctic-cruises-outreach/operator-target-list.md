## How to Approach

**For charter operators (A&K, PONANT, Scenic Eclipse):**
Pitch a dedicated Arctic Cruises charter departure or allotment. Lead with the seal conservation story + UNESCO Geopark. These operators build their own programmes — they don't sell someone else's brand.

**For distributor operators (Mundy, Rivervoyages):**
Pitch as a new product in their portfolio. Commission structure is the opener. FAM invitation is the closer.

**For German-language operators (Hapag-Lloyd, Phoenix, Nicko, A-ROSA):**
German copy exists (EN/DE operator materials ready). Lead with Scandinavia as familiar ground, Saimaa as the step beyond.

**For Saga:**
Frame as a new series: "Lake Saimaa Wilderness Voyage" as a branded Saga itinerary. They already expand fleet for 2027 — timing aligns perfectly.
