Järvisydän Resort

The Heart of Lake Saimaa

Where We Are

Järvisydän Resort is located in Rantasalmi, in the heart of the Finnish Lakeland on the shores of Lake Saimaa — Europe's fourth largest lake.

  • Helsinki 3.5 h by car
  • Savonlinna 45 min by car
  • Linnansaari Nat. Park 45 min by boat

Surrounded by 4,400 km² of pristine lake, islands, and ancient forest. Linnansaari National Park — home to the endangered Saimaa ringed seal — is our neighbor, reachable by boat directly from the resort.

One resort. Everything you need. On the most beautiful lake in Finland.

Linnansaari
National Park

One of Finland's most treasured national parks lies just 45 minutes by boat from Järvisydän. A pristine archipelago of forested islands, hidden coves, and crystal-clear waters.

Boat excursions depart directly from the resort — guided tours, private cruises, and the iconic M/S Puijo dinner cruise through the Saimaa waterway.

Saimaa Ringed Seal — one of the world's rarest seals, with only ~450 individuals. Our guided seal safaris offer a unique chance to observe them in their natural habitat.

Island experiences: Hiking trails, campfire lunch on a private island, kayak excursions through the archipelago, and sunset champagne cruises.

Lake Spa

Finland's most ecological Lake Spa — 5 pools and 6 saunas built into the natural lakeside landscape, partly carved into rock. Open sky, warm water, and the silence of nature.

5 pools — Outdoor heated pools overlooking Lake Saimaa, a cave pool built into natural rock, and indoor relaxation pools. Swim through stone passages from pool to pool.

6 saunas — Traditional Finnish smoke sauna, wood-burning saunas, and cold plunge pools. The authentic sauna ritual that Finland is famous for.

Treatments — Peat and herb treatments using local natural ingredients. Massage, facials, and holistic wellness packages.

Scenery Suites &
Kota Hotel

Scenery Suites (Maisemasviitit) — Our signature glass igloos. Large skylights frame the Northern Lights and midnight sun from your bed. Full-size luxury suites with fireplace, kitchenette, and private terrace. Available for 2 or 4 guests.

Kota Hotel — Three kota buildings with 7 rooms each, opened 2020. Skylight windows for stargazing, dark luxury interiors with lake panoramas. Each building has its own themed lounge.

Hotel, Hill Villas
& Panorama

Experience Hotel — Built from Finnish stone and old wood, the heart of the resort. Room types from Standard to VIP Suite, all with easy access to restaurants, spa, and activities. The practical base for larger groups and conferences.

Hillside Villas (Rinnehuvilat) — 8 private villas, 220 m² each, up to 10 guests. Five bedrooms with private bathrooms. Spacious, self-contained, ideal for families and executive groups.

Panorama Suites — On Panorama Hill with outdoor jacuzzi and private sauna. Available for 2 or 4 guests. Large windows frame the lake and forest in every season.

Wine Cellar &
Savonian Kota

Wine Cellar's Fire Menu — Fine dining in an atmospheric wine cellar. Seasonal ingredients cooked over open flame — an interactive fire-cooking experience that brings the Finnish campfire tradition into a unique underground setting. The scent of birch smoke and flavors drawn directly from forest and lake.

Savonian Kota Restaurant — The resort's iconic teepee restaurant seating 24 guests. Open-flame cooking under a traditional kota roof in the forest. Smoked meats, grilled fish, and campfire cuisine. Perfect for private group dining.

Fire cooking Local ingredients Kota tradition Group dining

Solitary & Kuru

Solitary — 9th Best Restaurant in Finland (2025)

An intimate fine dining concept by Chef Remi Tremouille. Ranked #9 in Finland by Viisi Tähteä (up from #30 in 2024). The menu changes with the seasons, the setting is Lake Saimaa, and every course tells the story of the region. 3-course or 5-course menus.

Kuru Resort

A separate adults-only lakeside area with its own suites, restaurant, and atmosphere. Contemporary Finnish cuisine built around local producers. Also home to Piikatyttö (breakfast & lunch) and Bistro (casual dinner). Kuru is the resort's culinary anchor.
