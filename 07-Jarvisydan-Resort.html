Where We Are

Järvisydän Resort is located in Rantasalmi, in the heart of the Finnish Lakeland on the shores of Lake Saimaa — Europe's fourth largest lake.

  • Helsinki 3.5 h by car
  • Helsinki → Mikkeli 3 h by train
  • Savonlinna 40 min by car
  • Linnansaari Nat. Park 1 km — by boat

Surrounded by 4,400 km² of pristine lake, islands, and ancient forest. Linnansaari National Park — home to the endangered Saimaa ringed seal — begins just 1 km from the resort, reached by boat from our own pier.

One resort. Everything you need. On the most beautiful lake in Finland.

Linnansaari
National Park

Linnansaari National Park begins just 1 km from Järvisydän — take the boat from our pier and you are inside the park. A pristine archipelago of forested islands, hidden coves, and crystal-clear waters.

Boat excursions depart directly from the resort — guided tours and private cruises through the Saimaa waterway archipelago.

Saimaa Ringed Seal — one of the world's rarest seals, with only ~450 individuals. Our guided seal safaris offer a unique chance to observe them in their natural habitat.

Island experiences: Hiking trails, campfire lunch on a private island, and kayak excursions through the archipelago.

National Park Seal safari Boat excursions Kayaking

Lake Spa

Järvisydän's Lake Spa brings together the best of Finnish wellness with the Saimaa lakefront as your backdrop. Open sky, open water, and the silence of nature.

Outdoor pools overlooking Lake Saimaa — swim while watching the seasons change across the lake and forest.

Sauna World — Traditional Finnish smoke sauna, wood-burning saunas, and cold plunge pools. The authentic sauna ritual that Finland is famous for.

Treatments — Peat and herb treatments using local natural ingredients. Massage, facials, and holistic wellness packages designed around the lakeside setting.
