The European Nature Cruise
Your Clients Are Looking For

7 nights aboard Finland's classic lake vessel through 4,400 islands of pristine UNESCO Lakeland. Endemic wildlife. Drinkable freshwater. Unbuilt shores. The product that hasn't appeared in your catalogue yet — because it hasn't existed until now.

Endemic & Irreplaceable
The Saimaa ringed seal exists nowhere else on Earth — approximately 500 individuals, found only here. A possible natural observation during the voyage is a differentiator no river cruise can match. UNESCO Geopark since 2021. 4,400 islands of unbuilt archipelago.
Premium at Accessible Price
European river cruises average €4,000–€8,000 pp / 7 nights. Saimaa Grand Cruise: €2,600 list — €2,080 net to you. Pristine wilderness instead of city riverbanks. Your margin is built in from day one. 20% early partner commission, limited window.
