## Business Concept

Arctic Cruises is a **public-private partnership cruise concept** on Lake Saimaa, positioning Finland's iconic lake system as a world-class multi-day cruise destination — comparable to Norwegian fjords or Croatian coastline routes.

**The core offer:** Guests cruise 3–9 days across the Saimaa labyrinth, stopping at island and coastal destinations, experiencing the Saimaa ringed seal, lakeside spa culture, local restaurants, and Finnish nature.

**Tagline:** *"Risteile maailman puhtaimmissa, jääkauden muovaamissa vesissä"*
("Cruise the world's purest, glacier-carved waters")

### Main Route (Lakeland Cruise)
Lappeenranta → Imatra → Puumala → Savonlinna → Rantasalmi (Järvisydän) → Varkaus → Kuopio

### Side Routes
- Mikkeli – Puumala – Mikkeli (via Anttola)
- Joensuu – Savonlinna – Joensuu (via Savonranta)
