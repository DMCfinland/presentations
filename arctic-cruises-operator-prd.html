10 Operational Requirements for Operators

What operators must communicate to guests

  • Vessel type: M/S Carelia is a day vessel — no overnight cabins. All nights are spent at shore-based resorts.
  • Departure point: All departures from Lappeenranta Harbour, not Helsinki. Transfer required and can be arranged via FinnConcierge.
  • Dress code: Smart casual throughout. No formal attire required.
  • Travel insurance: Mandatory. Operator responsibility to advise clients to purchase adequate cover before travel.
  • Optional activities: Kayak hire and Olavinlinna castle entry are not included. Recommend guests budget €30–50 for optional extras.
  • Fitness level: Moderate. Comfortable walking on uneven jetties and resort paths. No strenuous hiking.
  • Weather: May–June can be cool (8–16°C). July–August warm (18–24°C). September crisp and excellent for photography.

What operators must NOT promise guests

  • Seal sightings: Saimaa ringed seal sightings are possible, never guaranteed. Correct language: "You will cruise through seal habitat — sightings are a natural possibility." Never write or say "you will see a seal."
  • Specific resort at each stop: Resort assignment confirmed at booking; may change due to availability. Equivalent standard guaranteed. Do not name a specific resort in itinerary materials before booking confirmation.
  • Specific weather: Do not promise conditions. Use seasonal descriptors (crisp, golden, warm, light evenings) rather than temperature guarantees.
  • Wildlife encounters beyond seal: Osprey, white-tailed eagle, and other species may be observed — frame all as natural possibilities, not programme elements.
Seal language rule — zero tolerance: Any material promising seal sightings as guaranteed may create grounds for complaint and undermines the conservation positioning of the product. Use only: "You will cruise seal habitat."
Emergency contact during voyage: Laura Ilvonen · laura@finlanddmc.fi. Phone number provided to confirmed operators at booking confirmation. Response guaranteed within 2 hours during operational season.
