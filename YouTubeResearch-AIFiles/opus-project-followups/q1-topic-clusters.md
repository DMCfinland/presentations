## Meta-Takeaway (Opus's own conclusion)

> My honest self-assessment validates that a RAG approach would serve you better than full-corpus loading for most use cases. The 195-video consolidated file is too large for any model to process faithfully in a single pass. What I delivered was pattern extrapolation from ~15% of the data dressed as comprehensive synthesis.

> A better architecture: compressed summaries (2-3 sentences per video) as a navigational index, with full analyses available for RAG retrieval when specific topics arise. You get breadth from the index and depth from targeted retrieval. That's the hybrid approach the data itself recommends.
