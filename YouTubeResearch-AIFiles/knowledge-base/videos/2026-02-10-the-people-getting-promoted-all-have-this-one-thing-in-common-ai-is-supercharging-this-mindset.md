# The People Getting Promoted All Have This One Thing in Common (AI Is Supercharging this Mindset)

## Summary
The traditional career ladder is collapsing as AI automates entry-level work that once trained newcomers. The core strategic insight: **high agency—an internal locus of control combined with AI fluency—is now the only viable career strategy**. This isn't about confidence or positive thinking; it's about believing everything affecting your outcomes is within your control and using AI to rapidly overcome skill gaps. The people succeeding are those who treat AI as an extension of their capability, compress learning timelines from years to weeks, and relentlessly ship value. Entry-level hiring has collapsed 50%+ since 2019, but solo founders are building $80M+ companies in six months using AI. The gap between high and low agency people—once visible over decades—now manifests in months.

## 3. Strategic Engine

**How This Actually Works:**
High agency individuals adopt an internal locus of control (everything is inside their control circle), maintain an exceptional say-do ratio (collapsing the gap between intention and action), and use AI to rapidly patch skill gaps that would traditionally require years to address. They treat obstacles as "skill issues" to be solved through learning rather than external constraints. They ship constantly, learn through iteration, and create value that attracts opportunities rather than waiting for permission.

**Key Components:**
1. **Internal Locus of Control:** Everything affecting goals (promotion, comp, learning, location, family, career, all of it) goes inside the circle of control
2. **Say-Do Compression:** Immediate action on commitments—start now, not after planning or research
3. **AI as Capability Extension:** Using AI to learn skills (JavaScript, marketing, data analysis) and prototype/ship in weeks instead of years
4. **Skill-Issue Framing:** When encountering "that's impossible," responding with "that's a skill issue—I don't know how yet, but I can learn"
5. **Value Creation Obsession:** Constantly thinking about pushing more value out, not extracting value—trusting that high value creation returns to you

**Why This Works:**
- **Psychological:** Research shows people with internal locus of control show "greater persistence when facing challenges, higher academic achievement, better professional outcomes, more effective leadership, and stronger entrepreneurial activity"
- **Structural:** AI removes traditional barriers (coding, marketing, analysis skills) that required years/teams, enabling rapid skill acquisition
- **Compounding:** High agency creates a flywheel—shipping creates learning, learning enables more shipping, value creation attracts opportunities
- **Selection:** Organizations now explicitly select for AI-native high-agency people for leadership because they "will redefine what those roles mean"

## 9. System Health Metric (adapted from North Star Metric)

**What to Optimize For:**
**Say-Do Ratio × Shipping Velocity**

The compound metric of: (1) How quickly you act on stated intentions, and (2) How frequently you ship value into the world.

Specifically: Count of shipped projects/value created per month × inverse of time between stating intention and taking action.

**Why This Metric:**
- **Leading Indicator:** Predicts learning and capability growth before traditional metrics (titles, compensation) can catch up
- **Captures Both Components:** Say-do ratio ensures action bias; shipping velocity ensures consistent value creation
- **Self-Correcting:** If you're shipping frequently but not learning, quality/impact will reveal gaps; if say-do ratio is strong but shipping is low, you'll notice the disconnect
- **AI-Amplified:** This metric specifically captures the advantage AI provides (ability to ship faster with fewer blockers)
- **Directly Ties to Outcomes:** Research shows internal locus + action orientation → better outcomes; this metric measures both

**How to Measure:**
1. **Weekly Tracking:** Every Sunday, count: (a) Number of times you said you'd do something this week, (b) Number of times you did it immediately vs. delayed, (c) Number of shipped projects/value contributions
2. **Monthly Review:** Calculate: Projects shipped this month / Projects shipped last month (growth rate), and Average time from intention to action (should decrease)
3. **Quarterly Assessment:** Review expanded locus of control—what obstacles moved from "outside my circle" to "inside my circle"? What skills were acquired?

Practical example: If you say "I'll learn JavaScript" on Monday and have a working prototype by Friday = high say-do ratio + shipping velocity. If you say "I'll learn JavaScript" and spend 3 weeks researching courses = low say-do ratio, zero shipping velocity.

## 11. Application & Mental Model

### When to Use This Pattern

**Signal Conditions:**
- When traditional advancement paths are blocked or eliminated
- When skill gaps appear to block goals
- When facing rapid technological change that makes existing skills obsolete
- When building with limited resources (time, money, team)
- When needing to compress learning timelines
- When organizational structures are in flux
- When opportunities require capabilities you don't yet have
- When feeling stuck waiting for permission or external validation

**Applicability Test:**
Ask: "Is the obstacle I'm facing primarily a skill/knowledge gap, or a genuine external constraint beyond any reasonable control?" If it's primarily skill/knowledge, this pattern applies. If there's any way to influence the outcome through learning or action, this pattern applies.

### When NOT to Use This Pattern

**Contraindications:**
- **Genuine External Constraints:** True external forces (natural disasters, legal barriers, physical limitations) require different strategies
- **Collective Action Problems:** Some problems (systemic discrimination, labor rights) require collective organizing, not individual agency
- **Mental Health Crisis:** If anxiety from excessive internality is causing dysfunction, recalibrating locus of control may be necessary
- **Domain Expertise Critical:** Some fields (surgery, structural engineering) genuinely require years of supervised training—AI can't shortcut everything
- **Team Coordination Context:** When success requires deep coordination with others who don't share the high-agency mindset

**Red Flags:**
- If you find yourself blaming yourself for things genuinely outside your control
- If shipping velocity comes at the expense of learning or relationships
- If "that's a skill issue" becomes a way to avoid acknowledging systemic problems
- If action bias leads to thrashing rather than progress

### How to Apply to 1658 Holdings Companies

**Finland DMC Oy:**

- **Value Creation Focus:** Reorient metrics from hours worked to value created for clients. High agency people with AI can create disproportionate value—measure and reward that.

**General Principles:**

1. **Selection for Agency Over Credentials**
   - Redesign hiring to test for internal locus of control and say-do ratio

2. **AI as Standard Operating Equipment**

3. **Rapid Iteration Culture**
   - Reduce cycle time from intention to action across portfolio companies

   - Create feedback loops that reward learning through iteration

4. **Expand Internal Locus of Control**

   - Train leadership teams to frame challenges as skill issues rather than external constraints
   - Build capability maps: what skills would expand our control circle?

5. **Value Creation as Moat**
   - Orient portfolio companies toward creating exceptional value for customers as primary strategy
